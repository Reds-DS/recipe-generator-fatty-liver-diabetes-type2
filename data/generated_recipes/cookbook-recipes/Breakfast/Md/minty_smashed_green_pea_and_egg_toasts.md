# Minty Smashed Green Pea and Egg Toasts

![Minty Smashed Green Pea and Egg Toasts](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\minty_smashed_green_pea_and_egg_toasts.png)

Cool mint and a creamy yogurt-pea mash top crisp whole-grain toast in this savory, satisfying breakfast.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 cup (130 g) **Frozen green peas**
- 1/3 cup (75 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 2 tbsp (3 g) **Fresh mint**, finely chopped
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Garlic powder**
- 2 large (100 g) **Large eggs**
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **Liquid egg whites**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 4 slices (112 g) **100% whole-wheat bread**
- 2 tbsp (15 g) **Feta cheese**, crumbled
- 1 cup (144 g) **Fresh strawberries**, sliced

## Instructions
1. Boil water in a small pot over high heat. Add the peas, cook for 2 to 3 minutes until tender, and drain well.
2. Transfer the warm peas to a bowl. Add the yogurt, lemon juice, mint, and garlic powder, mashing with a fork until chunky but spreadable.
3. Whisk the whole eggs and egg whites together in a small bowl until fully blended.
4. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-low heat. Pour in the eggs and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring gently until soft curds form.
5. Toast the whole-wheat bread slices.
6. Spread the minted pea mash evenly over the warm toasts. Top with the scrambled eggs and crumbled feta.
7. Serve immediately alongside the sliced fresh strawberries.

## Variation
Swap the fresh mint for fresh dill or chopped basil.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; assembled toasts do not keep.
