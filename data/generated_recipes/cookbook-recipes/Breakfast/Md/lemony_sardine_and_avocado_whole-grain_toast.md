# Lemony Sardine and Avocado Whole-Grain Toast

![Lemony Sardine and Avocado Whole-Grain Toast](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\lemony_sardine_and_avocado_whole-grain_toast.png)

Start the day with a substantial hit of lean protein and fiber for steady energy and lasting fullness.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 4 slices (136 g) **Low-sodium sprouted whole-grain bread**, toasted
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Avocado**, pitted and peeled
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Fresh lemon zest**, finely grated
- 1.5 oz (40 g) **Baby arugula**
- 5 oz (150 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 2 cans (212 g) **No-salt-added canned sardines in water**, drained
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Crushed red pepper flakes**
- 5 oz (150 g) **Fresh strawberries**, hulled and halved

## Instructions
1. Toast the bread until golden and crisp.
2. Mash the avocado in a bowl with the lemon juice and lemon zest until combined but slightly chunky.
3. Spread the mashed avocado evenly across the toast slices.
4. Layer the baby arugula and halved cherry tomatoes over the avocado.
5. Break the drained sardines into large pieces with a fork and arrange them on top of the vegetables.
6. Sprinkle the crushed red pepper flakes evenly over the toasts.
7. Serve immediately with the fresh strawberries on the side.

## Variation
Trade the baby arugula for fresh baby spinach leaves.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; assembled toast does not keep.
