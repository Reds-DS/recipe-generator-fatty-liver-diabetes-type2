# Smoky Tomato Navy Beans and Eggs on Toast

![Smoky Tomato Navy Beans and Eggs on Toast](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\smoky_tomato_navy_beans_and_eggs_on_toast.png)

Start your morning with this satisfying, single-pan savory skillet. It reimagines sugary baked beans as a fast, high-fiber dish that provides steady energy until lunch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 8–12 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 oz (56 g) **Whole-wheat bread**
- 2 tbsp (30 g) **No-salt-added tomato paste**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 9 oz (260 g) **No-salt-added canned navy beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) **Water**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Apple cider vinegar**
- 1/8 tsp (1 g) **Salt**
- 2 cups (60 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 4 large **Large eggs**

## Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Toast the whole-wheat bread in a toaster.
2. Add the tomato paste, smoked paprika, and garlic powder to the skillet. Stir constantly for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant and slightly darkened.
3. Stir in the drained navy beans, water, apple cider vinegar, salt, and baby spinach. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes until the spinach wilts and the liquid forms a thick sauce.
4. Push the bean mixture aside to create wide wells in the skillet. Crack the eggs into the wells.
5. Cover the skillet and cook for 4 to 6 minutes. Check at 4 minutes to ensure the egg whites are completely set and the yolks remain soft.
6. Divide the toasted bread between plates. Scoop the eggs and smoky beans over the top to serve.

## Variation
Swap the navy beans for no-salt-added canned cannellini beans.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; cooked eggs and toast do not keep.
