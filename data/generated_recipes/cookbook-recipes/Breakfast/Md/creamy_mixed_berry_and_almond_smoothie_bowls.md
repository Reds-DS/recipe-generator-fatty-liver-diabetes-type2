# Creamy Mixed Berry and Almond Smoothie Bowls

![Creamy Mixed Berry and Almond Smoothie Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\creamy_mixed_berry_and_almond_smoothie_bowls.png)

This simple no-cook breakfast requires only a quick blitz in the blender. Silken tofu, frozen berries, and creamy almond butter combine into a remarkably thick base that keeps hunger away until lunch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 16 oz (450 g) **Silken tofu**, drained
- 3/4 cup (180 ml) **Unsweetened soy milk**
- 2 cups (280 g) **Frozen mixed berries**
- 1 medium (118 g) **Banana**, peeled and broken into pieces
- 2 tbsp (32 g) **Creamy almond butter**
- 2 tbsp (24 g) **Chia seeds**
- 1/2 cup (40 g) **Old-fashioned rolled oats**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Cinnamon**

## Instructions
1. Place the drained silken tofu, unsweetened soy milk, frozen mixed berries, banana, creamy almond butter, chia seeds, rolled oats, and cinnamon into a high-speed blender.
2. Blend on low speed to incorporate the dry and frozen ingredients into the liquids.
3. Increase the speed to high and blend until completely smooth and remarkably thick, stopping once or twice to scrape down the sides or use a blender tamper as needed.
4. Divide the thick mixture evenly between two bowls and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the creamy almond butter for unsweetened natural peanut butter.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
