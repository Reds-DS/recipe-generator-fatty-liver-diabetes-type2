# Mediterranean Salmon and Hummus Breakfast Wraps

![Mediterranean Salmon and Hummus Breakfast Wraps](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\mediterranean_salmon_and_hummus_breakfast_wraps.png)

This fast, no-cook breakfast is ready to eat as quickly as you can mix and assemble the ingredients. Flaky canned salmon and creamy hummus replace traditional breakfast meats to create a morning wrap that prevents mid-morning hunger.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 (90 g) **Whole-wheat tortillas, 6-inch fajita size**
- 8.5 oz (240 g) **Canned pink salmon (no-salt-added), rinsed and drained**, flaked
- 1/3 cup (80 g) **Plain hummus**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1 tbsp (3 g) **Fresh dill**, chopped
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 2 cups (60 g) **Baby spinach**
- 1 cup (100 g) **English cucumber**, diced
- 1/4 cup (40 g) **Red onion**, thinly sliced
- 1 1/2 cups (220 g) **Fresh mixed berries**

## Instructions
1. Flake the salmon with a fork in a medium bowl.
2. Stir the hummus, lemon juice, dill, and black pepper into the salmon until combined.
3. Lay the whole-wheat tortillas flat on a clean workspace and distribute the baby spinach evenly over each.
4. Spoon the salmon and hummus mixture evenly over the spinach.
5. Top with the diced cucumber and sliced red onion.
6. Roll the tortillas tightly to form firm wraps.
7. Serve the wraps immediately with the fresh berries on the side.

## Variation
Swap the baby spinach for fresh arugula or baby kale.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep well once assembled.
