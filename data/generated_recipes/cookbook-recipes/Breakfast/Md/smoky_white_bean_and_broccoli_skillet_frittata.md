# Smoky White Bean and Broccoli Skillet Frittata

![Smoky White Bean and Broccoli Skillet Frittata](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\smoky_white_bean_and_broccoli_skillet_frittata.png)

This simple skillet breakfast requires minimal prep and moves quickly from the stovetop to the oven. Folding white beans into a mix of eggs and broccoli creates a filling base that pairs well with fresh oranges.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 13–18 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 cup (80 g) **Red onion**, diced
- 1 1/2 cups (130 g) **Frozen chopped broccoli**
- 9 oz (260 g) **No-salt-added canned navy beans**, rinsed and drained
- 2 large (100 g) **Whole eggs**
- 1 cup (240 g) **Liquid egg whites**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1/2 oz (14 g) **Sharp cheddar cheese**, shredded
- 2 medium (260 g) **Fresh oranges**, peeled and segmented

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C. Whisk the whole eggs, liquid egg whites, smoked paprika, and black pepper in a medium bowl until combined.
2. Heat the olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat.
3. Add the red onion to the skillet and cook until softened, 3 to 4 minutes, checking at 3 minutes.
4. Stir in the frozen broccoli and drained navy beans. Cook until warmed through, 2 to 3 minutes, checking at 2 minutes.
5. Pour the egg mixture over the vegetables in the skillet. Cook undisturbed on medium-low heat until the edges begin to set, 3 to 4 minutes, checking at 3 minutes.
6. Sprinkle the cheddar cheese over the top and transfer the skillet to the oven. Bake until the center sets, 5 to 7 minutes, checking at 5 minutes.
7. Remove the skillet from the oven using an oven mitt. Slice the frittata in half and serve warm with the fresh orange segments.

## Variation
Swap the cheddar cheese for crumbled feta or goat cheese.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat briefly in the microwave.
