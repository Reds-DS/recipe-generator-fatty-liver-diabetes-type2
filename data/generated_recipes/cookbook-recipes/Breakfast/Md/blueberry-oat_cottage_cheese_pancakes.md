# Blueberry-Oat Cottage Cheese Pancakes

![Blueberry-Oat Cottage Cheese Pancakes](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\blueberry-oat_cottage_cheese_pancakes.png)

For a satisfying breakfast that feels like a weekend treat, these hearty pancakes deliver lasting morning fuel. Whole oats and cottage cheese blend into a protein-rich batter topped with an instant blueberry jam.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups (210 g) **Frozen blueberries**
- 1 tbsp (12 g) **Chia seeds**
- 1 cup (226 g) **No-salt-added low-fat cottage cheese**
- 1 cup (80 g) **Rolled oats**
- 3 large (150 g) **Large eggs**
- 3 tbsp (30 g) **Hemp hearts**
- 1 tsp (4 g) **Baking powder**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cinnamon**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**

## Instructions
1. Combine the frozen blueberries and chia seeds in a microwave-safe bowl, then microwave on high for 2 to 3 minutes until bubbling and bursting.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, oats, eggs, hemp hearts, baking powder, cinnamon, and vanilla extract in a blender on high until the batter is smooth.
3. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat and brush with the olive oil until shimmering.
4. Pour the batter into the pan in small circles to form the pancakes.
5. Cook the pancakes in batches for 4 to 6 minutes per batch, flipping halfway through when the edges appear set and the bottoms are golden brown.
6. Transfer the finished pancakes to a plate and repeat until all the batter is used.
7. Divide the warm pancakes between plates and spoon the thickened blueberry-chia jam over the top.

## Variation
Swap the frozen blueberries for frozen dark sweet cherries.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat pancakes in a dry skillet.
