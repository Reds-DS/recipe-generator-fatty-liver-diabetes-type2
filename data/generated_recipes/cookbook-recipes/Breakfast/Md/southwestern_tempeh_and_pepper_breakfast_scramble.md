# Southwestern Tempeh and Pepper Breakfast Scramble

![Southwestern Tempeh and Pepper Breakfast Scramble](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\southwestern_tempeh_and_pepper_breakfast_scramble.png)

This energizing breakfast replaces eggs with hearty crumbled tempeh to keep you full well past mid-morning. Smoky cumin and paprika bring bold Southwestern flavor to the skillet without relying on heavy sauces.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 8–11 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 6 oz (170 g) **Frozen bell pepper and onion blend**
- 9 oz (255 g) **Original tempeh**, crumbled
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 4 oz (113 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 3 oz (85 g) **100% whole-wheat tortillas**
- 2 oz (57 g) **Hass avocado**, diced
- 1/4 cup (4 g) **Fresh cilantro**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat until shimmering.
2. Add the frozen pepper and onion blend. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and any ice has evaporated.
3. Stir in the crumbled tempeh, ground cumin, and smoked paprika. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes until the tempeh is browned and aromatic.
4. Fold in the halved cherry tomatoes. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until they begin to soften.
5. Warm the whole-wheat tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave until pliable.
6. Divide the tempeh scramble between plates. Top with the diced avocado and chopped cilantro, and serve alongside the warm tortillas.

## Variation
Swap the cherry tomatoes for a handful of baby spinach.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; reheat in a skillet over medium.
