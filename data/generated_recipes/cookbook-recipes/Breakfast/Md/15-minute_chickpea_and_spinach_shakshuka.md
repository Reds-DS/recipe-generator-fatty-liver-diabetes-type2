# 15-Minute Chickpea and Spinach Shakshuka

![15-Minute Chickpea and Spinach Shakshuka](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\15-minute_chickpea_and_spinach_shakshuka.png)

This simple, single-skillet meal requires minimal prep and cooks completely on the stovetop. Eggs, chickpeas, and spinach simmer in a spiced tomato sauce for a hearty start to the day.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 13–19 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 medium **Yellow onion**, diced
- 2 cloves **Garlic**, minced
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Red wine vinegar**
- 14.5 oz (411 g) **No-salt-added canned diced tomatoes**, undrained
- 1/2 cup (130 g) **No-salt-added canned chickpeas**, rinsed and drained
- 3 cups (90 g) **Baby spinach**
- 4 **Large eggs**
- 1/2 cup (113 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 1 large (60 g) **Whole-wheat pita bread**, halved

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion and cook until softened, 3 to 4 minutes.
2. Stir in garlic, cumin, and smoked paprika. Cook, stirring constantly, for 1 to 2 minutes.
3. Pour in vinegar, tomatoes, and chickpeas. Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat, then lower heat to medium-low and simmer until the sauce thickens slightly, 4 to 5 minutes.
4. Stir spinach into the skillet in handfuls. Cook until the leaves are bright green and wilted, 1 to 2 minutes.
5. Form shallow wells in the hot tomato mixture using a spoon. Crack an egg into each well.
6. Cover the skillet with a lid and cook over low heat until the egg whites are firm but the yolks remain soft, 4 to 6 minutes.
7. Remove the skillet from the heat. Divide the warm shakshuka between plates and serve immediately with Greek yogurt and pita bread.

## Variation
Trade the spinach for chopped curly kale and simmer longer.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
