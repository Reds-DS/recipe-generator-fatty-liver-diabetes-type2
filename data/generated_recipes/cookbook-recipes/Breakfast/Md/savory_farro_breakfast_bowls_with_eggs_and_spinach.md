# Savory Farro Breakfast Bowls with Eggs and Spinach

![Savory Farro Breakfast Bowls with Eggs and Spinach](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\savory_farro_breakfast_bowls_with_eggs_and_spinach.png)

Starting your morning with intact grains and lean protein provides steady energy and lasting fullness. This single-skillet meal uses a convenient cooked grain pouch to keep prep time minimal.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 7–10 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1.5 cups (225 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked farro**
- 3 cups (90 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 2 large (100 g) **Whole eggs**
- 1 cup (240 ml) **Liquid egg whites**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Crushed red pepper flakes**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 3 tbsp (30 g) **Hemp hearts**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the whole eggs and liquid egg whites together in a small bowl.
2. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until they blister and soften, checking at 3 minutes.
3. Break up the farro in its pouch, then add it to the skillet along with the spinach. Stir and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the spinach wilts and the farro is warmed through.
4. Push the farro and vegetable mixture to the outer edges of the skillet to create an empty space in the center. Pour the egg mixture directly into the open space.
5. Cook the eggs for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring the center frequently with a spatula until softly scrambled and just set, checking at 3 minutes.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat. Fold the scrambled eggs into the farro mixture, then stir in the salt, black pepper, crushed red pepper flakes, and lemon juice.
7. Divide the mixture into serving bowls and sprinkle with the hemp hearts before serving.

## Variation
Swap the fresh baby spinach for chopped Tuscan kale.

## Storage
Keeps up to 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently in microwave.
