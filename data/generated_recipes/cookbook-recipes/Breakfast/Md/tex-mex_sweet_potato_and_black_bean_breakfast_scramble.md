# Tex-Mex Sweet Potato and Black Bean Breakfast Scramble

![Tex-Mex Sweet Potato and Black Bean Breakfast Scramble](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\tex-mex_sweet_potato_and_black_bean_breakfast_scramble.png)

Smoky cumin and vibrant salsa deliver bold southwestern flavor to this protein-packed, vegetable-forward breakfast. Pairing convenient frozen sweet potatoes with pantry black beans creates a hearty, high-fiber foundation that helps prevent mid-morning energy crashes.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 9–12 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Frozen diced sweet potatoes**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1 cup (260 g) **No-salt-added canned black beans**, rinsed and drained
- 10 oz (300 g) **Liquid egg whites**
- 2 large (100 g) **Whole eggs**
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) **Mild chunky salsa**
- 2.5 oz (75 g) **Avocado**, diced
- 1/4 cup (10 g) **Fresh cilantro**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Whisk the liquid egg whites and whole eggs together in a bowl until uniformly smooth.
2. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat with the olive oil until the oil shimmers.
3. Add the sweet potatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender and browned at the edges, 6 to 8 minutes. Check at 6 minutes.
4. Reduce the burner to medium. Stir the cumin and black beans into the skillet, tossing gently until the beans are warmed through.
5. Pour the egg mixture over the vegetables. Cook gently, pushing the eggs across the bottom with a silicone spatula as they begin to set, until fully cooked but still soft, 3 to 4 minutes. Check at 3 minutes.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat.
7. Divide the scramble between two plates and top evenly with the salsa, avocado, and cilantro before serving.

## Variation
Swap the black beans for rinsed, no-salt-added pinto beans.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
