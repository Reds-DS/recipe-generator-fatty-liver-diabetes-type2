# Savory Mediterranean Greek Yogurt Bowls

![Savory Mediterranean Greek Yogurt Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\savory_mediterranean_greek_yogurt_bowls.png)

Starting your morning with a generous serving of lean protein and fiber helps maintain steady energy right through to lunch. This fresh, zero-cook bowl relies on artichokes and whole-wheat pita to deliver lasting fullness without weighing you down.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups (340 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 1 cup (140 g) **English cucumber**, chopped
- 1 cup (150 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 8 oz (225 g) **Low-sodium canned artichoke hearts**, rinsed, drained, and quartered
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 tbsp (18 g) **Kalamata olives**, pitted and minced
- 2 tbsp (18 g) **Shelled pumpkin seeds (pepitas)**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Dried oregano**
- 2 pitas (120 g) **100% whole-wheat pita bread**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the Greek yogurt and lemon juice together in a small bowl until completely smooth.
2. Spread the yogurt mixture evenly across the bottoms of two wide, shallow bowls.
3. Arrange the chopped cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, and quartered artichoke hearts generously over the yogurt.
4. Drizzle the extra-virgin olive oil over the vegetables, then scatter the minced olives, pumpkin seeds, and dried oregano on top.
5. Toast the whole-wheat pitas in a toaster until slightly crisp and warm, then cut them into wedges.
6. Serve the bowls immediately with the warm pita wedges on the side for scooping.

## Variation
Swap the whole-wheat pita for a side of cooked quinoa.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; toast the pita just before serving.
