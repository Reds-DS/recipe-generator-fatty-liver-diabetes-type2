# Pinto Bean and Avocado Breakfast Tostadas

![Pinto Bean and Avocado Breakfast Tostadas](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\pinto_bean_and_avocado_breakfast_tostadas.png)

Eating a combination of plant and egg protein in the morning helps maintain steady energy and lasting fullness until lunch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 11–16 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1.5 tbsp (15 g) **Raw pepitas**
- 4 tortillas (100 g) **Yellow corn tortillas**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 1/2 cups (255 g) **No-salt-added canned pinto beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 4 cups (120 g) **Baby spinach**
- 2 large (100 g) **Whole eggs**
- 4 large (132 g) **Egg whites**
- 1/2 cup (110 g) **Fresh pico de gallo**
- 1/3 medium (50 g) **Avocado**, sliced

## Instructions
1. Place a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the raw pepitas and toast for 2 to 3 minutes until golden and fragrant, then transfer to a small bowl.
2. Warm the corn tortillas in the same skillet over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes per side until pliable and lightly toasted. Transfer to serving plates.
3. Add the extra-virgin olive oil, drained pinto beans, and ground cumin to the skillet over medium heat. Mash the beans roughly using a wooden spoon or silicone spatula for 3 to 4 minutes until warmed through, then divide evenly over the tortillas.
4. Add the baby spinach to the skillet and toss for 1 to 2 minutes until just wilted. Layer the spinach evenly over the mashed beans.
5. Whisk the whole eggs and egg whites together in a bowl. Pour into the skillet over medium-low heat and stir gently with a silicone spatula for 2 to 3 minutes until soft curds form and the eggs are set.
6. Spoon the scrambled eggs over the spinach. Top the tostadas evenly with the fresh pico de gallo, sliced avocado, and the toasted pepitas.

## Variation
Swap the baby spinach for thinly sliced fresh Tuscan kale.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep well once assembled.
