# Warm Blueberry and Vanilla Skyr Waffles

![Warm Blueberry and Vanilla Skyr Waffles](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\warm_blueberry_and_vanilla_skyr_waffles.png)

Packing plenty of protein and fiber, this comforting breakfast helps keep your morning energy steady until lunch. Tangy Skyr and warm berries replace sugary syrup for a naturally sweet start.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–5 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 4 waffles (140 g) **Frozen 100% whole-wheat waffles**
- 1 1/2 cups (210 g) **Frozen blueberries**
- 2 cups (454 g) **Nonfat plain Skyr**
- 1 tsp (4 ml) **Pure vanilla extract**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Ground cinnamon**
- 3 tbsp (30 g) **Hemp hearts**
- 1/4 cup (28 g) **Chopped walnuts**

## Instructions
1. Toast the whole-wheat waffles in a toaster or oven according to the package directions until golden and crisp.
2. Place the frozen blueberries in a small saucepan over medium heat while the waffles toast.
3. Cook the blueberries, stirring occasionally, for 4 to 5 minutes until the berries soften and burst. Remove the saucepan from the heat.
4. Whisk the Skyr, vanilla extract, and ground cinnamon in a medium bowl until smooth.
5. Divide the warm toasted waffles between two plates.
6. Spoon the vanilla Skyr mixture over the waffles, then ladle the warm blueberry sauce on top.
7. Sprinkle the hemp hearts and chopped walnuts over the waffles before serving.

## Variation
Swap the chopped walnuts for sliced almonds or toasted pecans.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
