# Fluffy Egg White Oatmeal with Berries and Walnuts

![Fluffy Egg White Oatmeal with Berries and Walnuts](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\fluffy_egg_white_oatmeal_with_berries_and_walnuts.png)

This simple stovetop technique transforms ordinary oats into a creamy, filling morning meal. Simmering rolled oats with liquid egg whites creates a thick bowl without relying on protein powders.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 7–10 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 cup (240 ml) **Water**
- 1 cup (240 ml) **Unsweetened almond milk**
- 1 cup (80 g) **Old-fashioned rolled oats**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cinnamon**
- 1/8 tsp (0.5 g) **Kosher salt**
- 1 3/4 cups (400 g) **Liquid egg whites**
- 1 1/2 cups (200 g) **Frozen mixed berries**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 1/4 cup (30 g) **Walnuts**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Combine the water, almond milk, oats, cinnamon, and salt in a medium saucepan. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil over medium-high heat.
2. Reduce the heat to low to maintain a gentle simmer. Cook the oats for 5 to 7 minutes, checking at 5 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed and the oats are tender.
3. Pour the liquid egg whites into the simmering oats in a slow stream while stirring vigorously to prevent scrambling.
4. Cook and stir the mixture for 2 to 3 minutes, checking at 2 minutes, until it becomes thick and the egg whites are opaque.
5. Remove the saucepan from the heat. Stir in the frozen berries and vanilla extract so the residual heat thaws the fruit.
6. Divide the warm oatmeal between two bowls. Top with the chopped walnuts before serving.

## Variation
Swap the chopped walnuts for sliced almonds or toasted pecans.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
