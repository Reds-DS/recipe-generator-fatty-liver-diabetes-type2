# Open-Faced Tomato and Spinach Egg Melts

![Open-Faced Tomato and Spinach Egg Melts](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Breakfast\IMG\open-faced_tomato_and_spinach_egg_melts.png)

This energizing breakfast transforms a heavy morning sandwich into a satisfying open-faced meal. A mix of eggs piled over whole wheat muffins with fresh tomato, mozzarella, and a side of strawberries keeps you full until lunch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 (114 g) **100% whole wheat English muffins**, split
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 cups (60 g) **Baby spinach**
- 2 large (100 g) **Whole eggs**
- 2/3 cup (150 ml) **Liquid egg whites**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 medium (120 g) **Fresh tomato**, sliced
- 1 oz (28 g) **Part-skim mozzarella cheese slices**, halved
- 3 cups (430 g) **Fresh strawberries**, hulled and halved

## Instructions
1. Toast the English muffin halves until golden and crisp.
2. Heat the olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add the spinach and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until wilted.
3. Whisk the whole eggs, liquid egg whites, and black pepper in a small bowl. Pour into the skillet with the spinach and scramble for 2 to 3 minutes until mostly set.
4. Lay the tomato slices and halved mozzarella cheese slices directly over the eggs. Cover the skillet, remove from the heat, and let sit for 1 minute until the cheese melts.
5. Divide the egg and tomato scramble over the open-faced muffin halves.
6. Serve immediately with the fresh strawberries on the side.

## Variation
Swap the strawberries for two cups of sliced fresh peaches.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
