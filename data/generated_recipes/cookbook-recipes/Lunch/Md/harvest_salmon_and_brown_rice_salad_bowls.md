# Harvest Salmon and Brown Rice Salad Bowls

![Harvest Salmon and Brown Rice Salad Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\harvest_salmon_and_brown_rice_salad_bowls.png)

Swapping heavy mayonnaise for a bright apple cider vinaigrette turns the classic Waldorf salad into a fresh grain bowl. This no-cook recipe relies on pantry staples for a simple, fiber-rich lunch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 15 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Apple cider vinegar**
- 1 tsp (5 g) **Dijon mustard**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 large (220 g) **Large crisp red apple**, cored and chopped
- 2 medium stalks (80 g) **Celery**, sliced
- 1 cup (150 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked brown rice**
- 8 oz (226 g) **No-salt-added canned pink salmon**, drained
- 4 cups (120 g) **Mixed baby greens**
- 3 tbsp (21 g) **Walnuts**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Whisk the extra-virgin olive oil, apple cider vinegar, Dijon mustard, salt, and black pepper in a large mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Add the chopped apple, celery, and brown rice to the vinaigrette and toss to coat the ingredients.
3. Flake the drained salmon with a fork, leaving some larger chunks intact.
4. Divide the mixed baby greens between two serving bowls.
5. Spoon the dressed rice, apple, and celery mixture over the greens.
6. Top each bowl with the flaked salmon and sprinkle with the chopped walnuts.

## Variation
Swap the pink salmon for drained no-salt-added canned tuna.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away to keep the greens crisp.
