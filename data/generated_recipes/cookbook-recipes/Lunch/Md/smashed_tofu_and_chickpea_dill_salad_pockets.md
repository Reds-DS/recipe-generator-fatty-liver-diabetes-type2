# Smashed Tofu and Chickpea Dill Salad Pockets

![Smashed Tofu and Chickpea Dill Salad Pockets](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\smashed_tofu_and_chickpea_dill_salad_pockets.png)

Creamy, tangy, and packed with fresh herbs, this zero-cook deli-style salad uses crumbled tofu and mashed chickpeas to deliver a satisfying, high-fiber lunch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 15 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Extra-firm tofu**, drained and patted dry
- 4.5 oz (130 g) **No-salt-added canned chickpeas**, rinsed and drained
- 5.3 oz (150 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 1.4 oz (40 g) **Celery**, finely diced
- 1 oz (30 g) **Red onion**, finely diced
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 2 tsp (10 g) **Dijon mustard**
- 2 tbsp (6 g) **Fresh dill**, finely chopped
- 2 pitas (128 g) **Whole-wheat pita breads (6-inch)**
- 2 oz (60 g) **Mixed salad greens**

## Instructions
1. Place the tofu and chickpeas in a medium mixing bowl.
2. Mash the mixture with a sturdy fork or potato masher until crumbled but still slightly chunky.
3. Fold the yogurt, celery, red onion, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, and dill into the mashed mixture until well combined.
4. Cut the pita breads in half to create pockets.
5. Line the inside of each pita pocket with the mixed salad greens.
6. Spoon the tofu and chickpea salad into the pockets and serve.

## Variation
Swap the fresh dill for chopped cilantro or flat-leaf parsley.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; store the salad and pita separately.
