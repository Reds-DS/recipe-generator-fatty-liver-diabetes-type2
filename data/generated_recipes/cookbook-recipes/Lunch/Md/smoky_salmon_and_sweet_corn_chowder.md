# Smoky Salmon and Sweet Corn Chowder

![Smoky Salmon and Sweet Corn Chowder](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\smoky_salmon_and_sweet_corn_chowder.png)

This hearty New England-inspired chowder relies on tender potatoes and a partial mash to build creaminess naturally. Salmon and sweet corn keep the bowl satisfying without the need for heavy cream.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 18–22 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Yellow onion**, finely chopped
- 4.2 oz (120 g) **Celery**, chopped
- 2 tsp (4 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 5.3 oz (150 g) **Baby red potatoes**, diced small
- 7 oz (200 g) **Frozen sweet corn**
- 1 cup (240 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 6 oz (170 g) **No-salt-added canned pink salmon**, drained and flaked
- 1 cup (240 ml) **Low-fat (1%) milk**
- 2 tbsp (6 g) **Fresh chives**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a medium pot over medium heat until shimmering, then add the onion and celery and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until softened.
2. Stir in the smoked paprika and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the potatoes, sweet corn, and vegetable broth, and bring the mixture to a gentle simmer over medium-high heat.
4. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover the pot, and simmer for 10 to 12 minutes, checking the potatoes with a fork to ensure they are tender.
5. Mash a portion of the potatoes and corn directly in the pot using a potato masher or the back of a large spoon to naturally thicken the chowder.
6. Stir in the flaked salmon and the milk, and warm the chowder over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes, ensuring the liquid does not boil.
7. Remove the pot from the heat, divide the chowder between bowls, and top with the fresh chives.

## Variation
Swap the pink salmon for drained no-salt-added canned white tuna.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
