# Garlicky Shrimp, Spinach, and White Bean Quesadillas

![Garlicky Shrimp, Spinach, and White Bean Quesadillas](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\garlicky_shrimp,_spinach,_and_white_bean_quesadillas.png)

This satisfying lunch comes together quickly when you need a filling meal to power through the afternoon. The creamy bean mash holds everything together while delivering a substantial fiber boost without heavy melted cheese.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Thaw time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 6 oz (170 g) **Frozen cooked peeled shrimp, untreated**, thawed, tails removed, and chopped
- 1 cup (260 g) **No-salt-added canned cannellini beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 2 tortillas (90 g) **Whole-wheat tortillas (8-inch)**
- 2 cups (60 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 3 tbsp (20 g) **Feta cheese**, crumbled
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**

## Instructions
1. Place the frozen shrimp in a bowl of cold water to thaw for 5 minutes. Drain the water, pull off any tails, and chop the shrimp.
2. Mash the cannellini beans, lemon juice, garlic powder, and dried oregano in a medium bowl until mostly smooth.
3. Lay the tortillas flat on a clean surface. Spread the bean mash evenly over one half of each tortilla to create a thick base.
4. Press the chopped shrimp, fresh spinach, and crumbled feta cheese into the bean mash. Fold the bare half over to close the quesadillas.
5. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and brush the surface with the extra-virgin olive oil.
6. Add the quesadillas to the skillet. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the bottoms are golden brown and crisp.
7. Flip the quesadillas and cook the other side for 2 to 3 minutes until crisp and the spinach wilts. Cut into wedges and serve.

## Variation
Trade the shrimp for cooked, shredded skinless chicken breast.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
