# Greek Chicken and Quinoa Stuffed Peppers

![Greek Chicken and Quinoa Stuffed Peppers](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\greek_chicken_and_quinoa_stuffed_peppers.png)

This satisfying lunch is perfect for a busy workday. Using the microwave to steam the bell peppers turns a weekend favorite into a fast, desk-friendly meal that provides steady afternoon energy.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 (320 g) **Large bell peppers (red or yellow)**, halved lengthwise and seeded
- 7 oz (200 g) **Shredded skinless rotisserie chicken breast**
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa**
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, quartered
- 2 cups (60 g) **Baby spinach**, roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp (30 g) **Kalamata olives**, pitted, rinsed, drained, and chopped
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 2 tbsp (30 g) **Crumbled feta cheese**

## Instructions
1. Place the bell pepper halves cut-side down in a microwave-safe dish with water. Cover with a microwave-safe lid and microwave on high for 4 to 6 minutes.
2. Remove the dish from the microwave and drain any excess water. Flip the peppers cut-side up to cool.
3. Whisk the extra-virgin olive oil, fresh lemon juice, and dried oregano together in a mixing bowl.
4. Add the shredded chicken, cooked quinoa, cherry tomatoes, baby spinach, and kalamata olives to the bowl. Toss the ingredients together until the spinach softens and the mixture is coated.
5. Spoon the chicken and quinoa filling into the steamed bell pepper halves.
6. Top each stuffed pepper with the crumbled feta cheese.
7. Serve the stuffed peppers immediately or let them cool entirely to pack in airtight containers.

## Variation
Swap the chicken for small, untreated cooked shrimp.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; enjoy cold or gently heated.
