# Ground Chicken and Kidney Bean Minestrone

![Ground Chicken and Kidney Bean Minestrone](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\ground_chicken_and_kidney_bean_minestrone.png)

A satisfying lunch or quick weeknight dinner, this one-pot soup brings Italian-inspired comfort without heavy meats. Lean chicken and fiber-rich kidney beans help stabilize afternoon energy levels.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 16–19 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 medium (80 g) **Yellow onion**, chopped
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic cloves**, minced
- 6 oz (170 g) **Lean ground chicken**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Italian seasoning blend**
- 1 1/2 cups (360 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 7 oz (200 g) **No-salt-added canned diced tomatoes**, with juices
- 1/2 cup (130 g) **No-salt-added canned dark red kidney beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1.5 oz (42 g) **Whole wheat short pasta (like rotini)**
- 1/2 cup (75 g) **Frozen chopped spinach**

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a medium pot over medium-high heat.
2. Add chopped onion, minced garlic, and ground chicken, and cook for 5 to 6 minutes while breaking the meat apart with a wooden spoon until browned.
3. Stir in Italian seasoning, chicken broth, diced tomatoes, kidney beans, and pasta.
4. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 9 to 11 minutes, checking the pasta for tenderness at 9 minutes.
5. Stir in frozen spinach and simmer for 2 more minutes until wilted and heated through.

## Variation
Swap the spinach for chopped kale or the chicken for turkey.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
