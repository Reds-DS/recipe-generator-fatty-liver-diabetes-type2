# Peanut-Lime Chicken and Whole-Wheat Noodle Salad

![Peanut-Lime Chicken and Whole-Wheat Noodle Salad](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\peanut-lime_chicken_and_whole-wheat_noodle_salad.png)

This incredibly simple lunch relies on a no-cook dressing and quick boiling to keep prep time short. It combines whole-wheat spaghetti, lean rotisserie chicken breast, and crisp pre-shredded cabbage slaw in a tangy peanut sauce.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–12 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **100% whole-wheat spaghetti**
- 6 oz (170 g) **Skinless rotisserie chicken breast**, shredded or chopped
- 6 oz (170 g) **Pre-shredded cabbage slaw mix**
- 4 oz (115 g) **English cucumber**, thinly sliced into half-moons
- 1.5 tbsp (24 g) **Natural peanut butter**, no added sugar
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lime juice**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Low-sodium soy sauce**
- 1 clove (3 g) **Fresh garlic**, minced
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Red pepper flakes**

## Instructions
1. Bring a medium pot of water to a rolling boil over high heat.
2. Add the spaghetti and cook for 10 to 12 minutes, or until tender.
3. Drain the pasta and rinse briefly under cold water to cool it down quickly. Transfer to a large mixing bowl.
4. Whisk the peanut butter, lime juice, soy sauce, garlic, red pepper flakes, and 1 tablespoon of warm water in a small bowl until smooth.
5. Add the shredded chicken, cabbage slaw mix, and sliced cucumber to the bowl with the cooled pasta.
6. Pour the peanut dressing over the salad and toss well to coat all the ingredients evenly.
7. Divide the noodle salad between two plates and serve immediately, or pack for lunch.

## Variation
Swap the chicken for shelled edamame to make it vegetarian.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; best enjoyed cold or at room temperature.
