# Mediterranean Tuna and White Bean Pita Pockets

![Mediterranean Tuna and White Bean Pita Pockets](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\mediterranean_tuna_and_white_bean_pita_pockets.png)

Mashing white beans into tuna provides lean protein and steady carbohydrates to keep you full well into the afternoon. Skipping traditional mayonnaise keeps saturated fat low while letting the bright flavors of lemon and fresh vegetables shine.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 15 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 7 oz (200 g) **No-salt-added canned chunk light tuna in water**, rinsed and drained
- 5 oz (150 g) **No-salt-added canned cannellini beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 1/2 tbsp (22 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1/4 cup (10 g) **Fresh parsley**, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup (30 g) **Red onion**, finely chopped
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Cucumber**, diced
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, quartered
- 2 medium (128 g) **Whole-wheat pitas**

## Instructions
1. Place the drained cannellini beans in a medium mixing bowl and mash with a fork until mostly smooth, leaving a few chunks for texture.
2. Add the drained tuna to the bowl with the mashed beans. Pour in the olive oil and lemon juice, sprinkle with black pepper, and mix thoroughly.
3. Fold the chopped parsley, red onion, cucumber, and cherry tomatoes into the tuna mixture until evenly combined.
4. Cut the whole-wheat pitas in half to create pockets and pry each one open.
5. Spoon the filling into the pita pockets and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the canned tuna for flaked, untreated canned pink salmon.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; assemble in fresh pitas just before serving.
