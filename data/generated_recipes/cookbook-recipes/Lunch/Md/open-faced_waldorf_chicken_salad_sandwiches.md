# Open-Faced Waldorf Chicken Salad Sandwiches

![Open-Faced Waldorf Chicken Salad Sandwiches](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\open-faced_waldorf_chicken_salad_sandwiches.png)

Swapping mayonnaise for Greek yogurt transforms this deli classic into a practical weekday lunch. Fresh grapes add natural sweetness and crunch, creating a bright salad that pairs perfectly with whole-grain bread.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (115 g) **Plain nonfat Greek yogurt**
- 2 tsp (10 g) **Dijon mustard**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 7 oz (200 g) **No-salt-added canned chicken breast**, rinsed and drained
- 1/2 cup (80 g) **Celery**, diced
- 1 cup (150 g) **Red grapes**, halved
- 1 oz (28 g) **Walnuts**, chopped
- 4 slices (140 g) **Dense whole-grain bread**
- 2 cups (40 g) **Baby arugula**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the yogurt, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, and black pepper together in a medium mixing bowl.
2. Flake the drained chicken into the bowl using a fork.
3. Fold the diced celery, halved grapes, and chopped walnuts into the yogurt mixture until evenly coated.
4. Lay the whole-grain bread slices flat on serving plates.
5. Divide the arugula evenly across the bread slices.
6. Spoon the chicken salad over the arugula and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the chopped walnuts for unsalted chopped pecans or almonds.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; add the arugula just before serving.
