# Caribbean Shrimp, Mango, and Quinoa Bowls

![Caribbean Shrimp, Mango, and Quinoa Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\caribbean_shrimp,_mango,_and_quinoa_bowls.png)

This incredibly fast bowl requires only a quick sear for the shrimp. Sweet mango balances zesty lime, mild quinoa, and hearty black beans for a refreshing, flavor-packed meal.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 3–4 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8 oz (225 g) **Raw peeled shrimp (tail-off), untreated**
- 4.5 oz (130 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked plain quinoa**
- 4.6 oz (130 g) **No-salt-added canned black beans**, rinsed and drained
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Fresh mango**, diced
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Red bell pepper**, diced
- 1 oz (28 g) **Scallions**, thinly sliced
- 0.3 oz (8 g) **Fresh cilantro**, roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Lime juice**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Crushed red pepper flakes**

## Instructions
1. Heat half the olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add the shrimp and red pepper flakes, cooking for 3 to 4 minutes, flipping once, until the shrimp are pink and opaque throughout.
3. Remove the skillet from the heat and set aside.
4. Whisk the lime juice and the remaining olive oil together in a large mixing bowl.
5. Add the quinoa, black beans, mango, bell pepper, scallions, and cilantro to the bowl, tossing gently to coat the ingredients evenly in the dressing.
6. Divide the quinoa and mango salad between two shallow bowls, top with the seared shrimp, and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the shrimp for diced boneless, skinless chicken breast.

## Storage
Keeps up to 2 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
