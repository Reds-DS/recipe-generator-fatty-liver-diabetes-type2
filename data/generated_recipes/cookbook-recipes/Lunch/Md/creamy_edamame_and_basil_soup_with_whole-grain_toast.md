# Creamy Edamame and Basil Soup with Whole-Grain Toast

![Creamy Edamame and Basil Soup with Whole-Grain Toast](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\creamy_edamame_and_basil_soup_with_whole-grain_toast.png)

Velvety and vibrant, this bright green soup transforms convenient freezer staples into a deeply savory, herb-forward bowl.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 6–9 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 10.5 oz (300 g) **Frozen shelled edamame**
- 2 cups (480 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 2 cups (60 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 1/2 cup (15 g) **Fresh basil leaves**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1/8 tsp (0.5 g) **Salt**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Unsalted pumpkin seeds (pepitas)**
- 2 slices (70 g) **Hearty whole-grain bread**, toasted

## Instructions
1. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a medium pot over medium heat until shimmering. Add the garlic and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant and softened. Check at 1 minute.
2. Add the frozen shelled edamame and low-sodium vegetable broth. Bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat, then reduce the heat to low. Simmer for 5 to 7 minutes until the edamame is thoroughly heated and tender. Check at 5 minutes.
3. Remove the pot from the heat. Stir in the fresh baby spinach, fresh basil leaves, fresh lemon juice, salt, and black pepper until the spinach wilts from the residual heat.
4. Transfer the mixture carefully to a blender, or use an immersion blender directly in the pot. Blend until the soup is velvety and completely smooth.
5. Divide the soup between two bowls. Garnish with the unsalted pumpkin seeds and serve immediately with the whole-grain bread on the side.

## Variation
Swap the pumpkin seeds for toasted slivered almonds.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated in an airtight container; reheat gently.
