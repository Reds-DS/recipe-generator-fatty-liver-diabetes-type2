# Southwestern Turkey, Corn, and White Bean Chili

![Southwestern Turkey, Corn, and White Bean Chili](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\southwestern_turkey,_corn,_and_white_bean_chili.png)

A hearty serving of lean protein and dietary fiber helps keep your energy steady through the afternoon. Using low-sodium canned staples means this earthy, comforting bowl is ready without the traditional all-day simmer.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 13–16 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 cup (80 g) **Yellow onion**, chopped
- 8 oz (227 g) **Lean ground turkey (93% lean)**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1/2 tsp (1.5 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 cup (170 g) **No-salt-added canned cannellini beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup (130 g) **Frozen sweet corn**
- 4 oz (113 g) **Low-sodium diced green chiles**, drained
- 1 cup (240 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **Water**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lime juice**
- 2 tbsp (5 g) **Fresh cilantro**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a medium pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add chopped onion and cook until it starts to soften, about 3 minutes.
2. Add turkey, cumin, and garlic powder to the pot. Cook, breaking meat apart with a spoon, until no longer pink, about 4 to 5 minutes.
3. Stir in drained beans, corn, drained green chiles, broth, and water. Bring mixture to a rolling boil.
4. Reduce heat to medium-low to maintain a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered to blend flavors, about 6 to 8 minutes.
5. Remove pot from heat. Stir in lime juice and chopped cilantro just before serving.

## Variation
Swap the ground turkey for an equal amount of ground chicken.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat on the stovetop or microwave.
