# Tuscan Chicken and Smashed White Bean Wraps

![Tuscan Chicken and Smashed White Bean Wraps](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\tuscan_chicken_and_smashed_white_bean_wraps.png)

This satisfying lunch is perfect to assemble ahead of time and eat at your desk. Smashed cannellini beans stand in for mayonnaise to bind this herb-packed chicken salad while keeping you full for hours.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (130 g) **No-salt-added canned cannellini beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 1/2 tsp (2 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 5.5 oz (150 g) **Skinless rotisserie chicken breast**, chopped
- 1/4 cup (20 g) **Sun-dried tomatoes (dry-packed)**, chopped
- 1/2 cup (100 g) **No-salt-added canned artichoke hearts (water-packed)**, rinsed, drained, and roughly chopped
- 2 large (120 g) **100% whole-wheat tortillas**
- 2 cups (40 g) **Fresh baby spinach**

## Instructions
1. Place the beans in a medium bowl and mash with a fork until mostly smooth.
2. Stir the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, and oregano into the mashed beans until combined.
3. Fold the chicken, sun-dried tomatoes, and artichoke hearts into the bean mixture until coated.
4. Lay the whole-wheat tortillas flat on a clean work surface and divide the spinach between them.
5. Spoon the chicken and bean salad over the spinach and spread into an even layer.
6. Fold the sides of each tortilla inward and roll tightly from the bottom to form a wrap.
7. Slice the wraps in half diagonally and serve.

## Variation
Swap the artichoke hearts for chopped roasted red peppers.

## Storage
Keeps up to 2 days refrigerated; enjoy cold.
