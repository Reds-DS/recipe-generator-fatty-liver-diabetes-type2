# Balsamic Steak and Artichoke Farro Bowls

![Balsamic Steak and Artichoke Farro Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\balsamic_steak_and_artichoke_farro_bowls.png)

Most bistros serve steak with heavy fries, but pairing it with hearty farro and fiber-rich artichokes creates a more balanced meal.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Rest time:** 3 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 10 oz (285 g) **Lean sirloin steak strips**, cut into 1-inch strips
- 8.5 oz (240 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked farro pouch**
- 1 can, drained (240 g) **Canned artichoke hearts (in water, no-salt-added)**, halved
- 1 cup (150 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 2 cups (60 g) **Baby arugula**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Balsamic vinegar**
- 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp (20 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**, divided
- 1 tsp (5 g) **Dijon mustard**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the balsamic vinegar, olive oil, Dijon mustard, garlic powder, and black pepper in a large mixing bowl to create a dressing.
2. Add the artichoke hearts, cherry tomatoes, and arugula to the bowl. Toss to coat, then divide the salad mixture into serving bowls.
3. Heat the farro according to the pouch instructions. Spoon the warm grain over the salad bases.
4. Pat the steak strips dry with a paper towel. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
5. Add the steak in a single layer. Sear for 4 to 6 minutes, flipping halfway through, until browned.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat and let the meat rest in the pan to retain its juices.
7. Top each bowl with the rested steak strips and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the sirloin steak strips for boneless, skinless chicken breast.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pack the steak and farro separately.
