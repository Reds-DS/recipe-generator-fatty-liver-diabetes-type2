# Spiced Tilapia and Orange Quinoa Bowls

![Spiced Tilapia and Orange Quinoa Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\spiced_tilapia_and_orange_quinoa_bowls.png)

This quick bowl relies on a fast stovetop sear and a ready-to-eat grain base for an easy dinner. Warm, spiced tilapia fillets pair perfectly with juicy navel orange segments and peppery arugula for a satisfying meal.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 6–8 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8 oz (225 g) **Tilapia fillets**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Kosher salt**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**, divided
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa**, from a microwaveable pouch
- 3 oz (85 g) **Baby arugula**
- 1 large (150 g) **Navel orange**, peeled and segmented
- 1/2 medium (70 g) **Avocado**, sliced
- 3 medium (45 g) **Radishes**, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lime juice**

## Instructions
1. Pat the tilapia dry with a paper towel. Rub both sides evenly with the smoked paprika, ground cumin, and salt.
2. Heat a portion of the olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add the tilapia and cook for 3 to 4 minutes per side, flipping halfway through, until the crust is golden and the fish flakes easily with a fork.
3. Warm the cooked quinoa in the microwave according to the pouch instructions.
4. Whisk the lime juice and the remaining olive oil in a small bowl to create a dressing.
5. Divide the arugula and warmed quinoa between two wide, shallow bowls.
6. Arrange the seared tilapia, orange segments, sliced avocado, and radishes over the greens and grains.
7. Drizzle the lime dressing evenly across both bowls just before serving.

## Variation
Swap the tilapia for peeled shrimp and sear until pink.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; greens will wilt if stored dressed.
