# Southwestern Chicken and Black Bean Mason Jar Salad

![Southwestern Chicken and Black Bean Mason Jar Salad](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\southwestern_chicken_and_black_bean_mason_jar_salad.png)

This desk-friendly salad builds a robust meal for busy weekdays without relying on heavy bottled dressings. The layered approach keeps the greens crisp until you are ready to shake and eat.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 15 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lime juice**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/4 cup (4 g) **Fresh cilantro**, finely chopped
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Kosher salt**
- 1 cup (150 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 1/2 cup (130 g) **Canned black beans, no-salt-added**, rinsed and drained
- 1/2 cup (70 g) **Frozen whole kernel corn**, thawed
- 1/2 cup (90 g) **Plain quinoa**, cooked and cooled
- 6 oz (170 g) **Cooked chicken breast**, chopped
- 3 cups (140 g) **Romaine lettuce**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Whisk the lime juice, olive oil, cilantro, cumin, and salt in a small bowl to make the dressing.
2. Divide the dressing into the bottom of large mason jars or tall storage containers.
3. Layer the halved cherry tomatoes directly into the dressing.
4. Add the rinsed black beans, thawed corn, and cooked quinoa in even layers on top of the tomatoes.
5. Place the chopped cooked chicken over the grains and beans.
6. Pack the chopped romaine lettuce into the top of the jars and secure with lids.
7. Shake the jars before serving to distribute the dressing over the greens.

## Variation
Swap the cooked chicken for drained canned low-sodium tuna.

## Storage
Keeps 2 to 3 days refrigerated in sealed jars.
