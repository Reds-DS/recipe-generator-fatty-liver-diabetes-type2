# Deconstructed Spicy Shrimp Sushi Bowls

![Deconstructed Spicy Shrimp Sushi Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\deconstructed_spicy_shrimp_sushi_bowls.png)

When you want a satisfying workday lunch without a midday crash, this bowl delivers the exact flavors of a spicy shrimp roll. Pre-cooked shrimp and a quick Greek yogurt dressing keep it filling and entirely effortless.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8 oz (227 g) **Frozen cooked shrimp, peeled and tail-off (untreated)**
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked brown rice pouch**
- 1/4 cup (60 g) **Plain nonfat Greek yogurt**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Sriracha sauce**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Reduced-sodium soy sauce**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Unseasoned rice vinegar**
- 1/2 (150 g) **English cucumber**, chopped
- 1/2 cup (50 g) **Bagged matchstick carrots**
- 1 small (100 g) **Avocado**, diced
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Toasted sesame seeds**

## Instructions
1. Place the frozen shrimp in a colander and run under cold water for 5 minutes to thaw, then drain well and pat dry.
2. Heat the ready-to-eat brown rice pouch in the microwave according to the package directions until softened and warm.
3. Whisk the yogurt, sriracha, soy sauce, and rice vinegar in a small bowl until completely smooth.
4. Divide the warmed brown rice evenly between serving bowls.
5. Top the rice with the thawed shrimp, chopped cucumber, diced avocado, and matchstick carrots.
6. Drizzle the spicy yogurt dressing evenly over the assembled bowls.
7. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the shrimp for flaked no-salt-added canned pink salmon.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; add the avocado just before serving.
