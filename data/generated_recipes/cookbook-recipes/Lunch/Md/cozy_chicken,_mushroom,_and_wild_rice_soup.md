# Cozy Chicken, Mushroom, and Wild Rice Soup

![Cozy Chicken, Mushroom, and Wild Rice Soup](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\cozy_chicken,_mushroom,_and_wild_rice_soup.png)

Relying on a single pot and ready-to-use ingredients, this quick-simmering soup is simple to prepare. It pairs lean chicken breast, earthy mushrooms, and wild rice for a comforting, balanced bowl.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 13–18 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 6 oz (170 g) **Thin-sliced boneless, skinless chicken breasts**, chopped into bite-sized pieces
- 8 oz (225 g) **Cremini mushrooms**, sliced
- 1 1/2 cups (165 g) **Carrots**, pre-shredded
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Fresh thyme**, chopped
- 3 cups (720 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 1 1/2 cups (210 g) **Pre-cooked brown and wild rice blend**, from a ready-to-eat pouch
- 3 cups (90 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Apple cider vinegar**

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a pot over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add chicken and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, checking at 3 minutes, until lightly browned.
2. Stir in mushrooms and carrots. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, checking at 4 minutes, until the mushrooms soften and release their liquid.
3. Stir in garlic powder and thyme. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, checking at 1 minute, until fragrant.
4. Pour in broth and wild rice. Bring to a rolling boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 5 to 7 minutes, checking at 5 minutes, until heated through.
5. Remove the pot from the heat. Stir in spinach and vinegar until the leaves are wilted, then portion into bowls.

## Variation
Swap the chicken for a can of rinsed cannellini beans.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
