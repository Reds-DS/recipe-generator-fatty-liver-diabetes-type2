# Smoky Shrimp, Tomato, and Whole Grain Stew

![Smoky Shrimp, Tomato, and Whole Grain Stew](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\smoky_shrimp,_tomato,_and_whole_grain_stew.png)

Smoked paprika and a splash of sherry vinegar deliver the savory depth of a classic Spanish seafood stew without the excess saturated fat. Adding a ready-to-eat grain pouch directly into the simmering broth builds a hearty, thick texture instantly.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 11–16 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 1/2 cups (150 g) **Frozen bell pepper and onion blend**
- 2 tsp (4 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 cup (240 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 1 can (14.5 oz / 411 g) **No-salt-added diced tomatoes**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Ready-to-eat brown rice and quinoa pouch**
- 9 oz (250 g) **Frozen pre-cooked shrimp (untreated / no salt added)**, thawed
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Sherry vinegar**
- 1/4 cup (15 g) **Fresh parsley**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a medium pot over medium-high heat. Add the frozen pepper and onion blend, and sauté until softened, 3 to 4 minutes.
2. Stir in the smoked paprika and garlic powder. Cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. Pour in the vegetable broth and diced tomatoes with their juices. Bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat, reduce the heat to low, and simmer until the tomatoes break down slightly, 5 to 6 minutes.
4. Stir the brown rice and quinoa into the simmering broth. Cook until the grains absorb some of the liquid and warm through, 2 to 3 minutes.
5. Fold in the thawed shrimp and sherry vinegar. Simmer until the shrimp are hot and opaque, 1 to 2 minutes.
6. Remove the pot from the heat immediately to prevent the shrimp from overcooking. Stir in the parsley and portion the stew into warm bowls.

## Variation
Trade the shrimp for diced, roasted chicken breast pieces.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
