# Mediterranean Artichoke, Chicken, and Farro Bowls

![Mediterranean Artichoke, Chicken, and Farro Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\mediterranean_artichoke,_chicken,_and_farro_bowls.png)

This bright, lemony bowl delivers steady afternoon energy by pairing lean protein with high-fiber farro and vegetables. Relying on simple pantry staples helps you assemble a satisfying lunch without turning on the stove.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked farro pouch**
- 10 oz (280 g) **No-salt-added canned chicken breast**, drained
- 8.5 oz (240 g) **No-salt-added canned quartered artichoke hearts (in water)**, rinsed and drained
- 5 oz (150 g) **Cherry tomatoes**
- 1/4 cup (15 g) **Fresh flat-leaf parsley**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1/2 tsp (1.5 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Kosher salt**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**

## Instructions
1. Warm the farro according to the package instructions.
2. Halve the cherry tomatoes and chop the parsley while the farro warms.
3. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper together in a large mixing bowl.
4. Add the warm farro, drained chicken, drained artichoke hearts, tomatoes, and parsley to the bowl.
5. Toss to combine, breaking the chicken apart into bite-sized chunks.
6. Divide the mixture between plates to serve immediately, or pack into containers for a cold lunch.

## Variation
Trade the canned chicken for drained light tuna in water.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated in an airtight container; serve cold.
