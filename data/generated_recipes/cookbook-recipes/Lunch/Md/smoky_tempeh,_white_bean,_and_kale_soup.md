# Smoky Tempeh, White Bean, and Kale Soup

![Smoky Tempeh, White Bean, and Kale Soup](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\smoky_tempeh,_white_bean,_and_kale_soup.png)

Crumbled tempeh acts as a hearty, savory stand-in for traditional ground sausage in this fast, one-pot soup. It delivers plant-based protein without the saturated fat found in cured meats.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 12–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 6 oz (170 g) **Tempeh**, crumbled
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 2 cups (480 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 8 oz (225 g) **No-salt-added canned diced tomatoes**, undrained
- 7 oz (200 g) **No-salt-added canned cannellini beans**, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups (42 g) **Fresh kale**, stemmed and chopped
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Red wine vinegar**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**

## Instructions
1. Warm extra-virgin olive oil in a medium pot over medium-high heat.
2. Add crumbled tempeh and sauté for 4 to 5 minutes until lightly browned.
3. Stir in smoked paprika and garlic powder, cooking for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Pour in vegetable broth, diced tomatoes, and cannellini beans, and bring the mixture to a boil.
5. Reduce heat to low, stir in chopped kale, and cover the pot.
6. Simmer for 5 to 7 minutes, checking at 5 minutes to ensure the kale is tender.
7. Remove from heat, stir in red wine vinegar and salt, and ladle the soup into bowls.

## Variation
Swap the chopped fresh kale for fresh baby spinach.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
