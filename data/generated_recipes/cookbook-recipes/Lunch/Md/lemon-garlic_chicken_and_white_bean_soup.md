# Lemon-Garlic Chicken and White Bean Soup

![Lemon-Garlic Chicken and White Bean Soup](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\lemon-garlic_chicken_and_white_bean_soup.png)

This comforting soup relies on fiber-rich white beans and lean ground chicken to provide steady energy and keep you full. Lemon juice and aromatic herbs build deep flavor without relying on heavy cream or extra salt.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 14–18 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 8 oz (225 g) **93% lean ground chicken**
- 1 cup (115 g) **Frozen mirepoix blend**
- 3 cloves (9 g) **Fresh garlic**, minced
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried Italian seasoning**
- 2 cups (480 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 9 oz (255 g) **No-salt-added canned Great Northern beans**, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups (60 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**

## Instructions
1. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a medium pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add the ground chicken and break it apart with a wooden spoon. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until no longer pink.
3. Stir in the frozen mirepoix blend and minced garlic. Sauté for 3 to 4 minutes until the vegetables soften and smell fragrant.
4. Mix in the dried Italian seasoning, low-sodium chicken broth, and rinsed Great Northern beans. Bring the liquid to a rolling boil over medium-high heat.
5. Reduce the heat to medium-low to maintain a gentle simmer. Cover the pot partially and cook for 8 to 10 minutes to allow the flavors to combine.
6. Remove the pot from the heat. Stir in the fresh baby spinach and fresh lemon juice, folding until the spinach wilts.
7. Ladle the soup into bowls and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the ground chicken for lean ground turkey or tofu.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
