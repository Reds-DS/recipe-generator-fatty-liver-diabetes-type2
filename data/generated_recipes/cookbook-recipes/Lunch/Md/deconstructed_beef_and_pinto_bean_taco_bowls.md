# Deconstructed Beef and Pinto Bean Taco Bowls

![Deconstructed Beef and Pinto Bean Taco Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\deconstructed_beef_and_pinto_bean_taco_bowls.png)

Turning a traditional taco into a bowl makes it easy to add extra fiber with crisp greens and brown rice. These base ingredients perfectly support the smoky, savory flavors of the lean beef.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 6–9 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 10 oz (280 g) **96% lean ground beef**
- 1 tbsp (7 g) **Salt-free taco seasoning**
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) **Low-sodium beef broth**
- 1 cup (140 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked brown rice (from a pouch)**
- 4 cups (150 g) **Romaine lettuce**, chopped
- 7 oz (200 g) **No-salt-added canned pinto beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1/2 cup (120 g) **Fresh pico de gallo**
- 2.5 oz (75 g) **Avocado**, diced
- 1/4 cup (10 g) **Fresh cilantro**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat extra-virgin olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add ground beef and salt-free taco seasoning. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes, checking at 5 minutes, while breaking the meat apart with a wooden spoon.
3. Pour in beef broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Simmer for 1 to 2 minutes, checking at 1 minute, then remove from heat.
4. Warm brown rice in the microwave according to package directions.
5. Divide chopped romaine lettuce between wide, shallow bowls.
6. Top the lettuce with the warmed brown rice, rinsed pinto beans, and seasoned beef.
7. Garnish each bowl with fresh pico de gallo, diced avocado, and chopped cilantro before serving.

## Variation
Swap the ground beef for extra-lean ground turkey breast.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; store the avocado and greens separately.
