# Curried Chicken, Apple, and Walnut Salad Pockets

![Curried Chicken, Apple, and Walnut Salad Pockets](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\curried_chicken,_apple,_and_walnut_salad_pockets.png)

This satisfying lunch is perfect for busy afternoons, swapping heavy mayonnaise for a spiced Greek yogurt dressing. Crisp apples and walnuts add a pleasant crunch, while whole-wheat pitas provide sustained energy.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 15 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup (75 g) **Plain nonfat Greek yogurt**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Mild curry powder**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**
- 6 oz (170 g) **Skinless rotisserie chicken breast**, shredded or chopped
- 1 medium (150 g) **Fuji or Honeycrisp apple**, cored and chopped
- 2 stalks (80 g) **Celery**, chopped
- 3 tbsp (21 g) **Chopped walnuts**
- 2 large (120 g) **Whole-wheat pita pockets**, halved
- 2 cups (60 g) **Baby spinach**

## Instructions
1. Mix the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, curry powder, and black pepper in a medium bowl.
2. Fold the shredded chicken, chopped apple, celery, and walnuts into the yogurt mixture until thoroughly coated.
3. Open the halved pita pockets gently to avoid tearing the seams.
4. Stuff each pita half with the baby spinach leaves.
5. Spoon the chicken salad into the pitas over the spinach and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the chopped walnuts for sliced almonds or toasted pecans.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; assemble pitas with spinach just before serving.
