# Blistered Tomato and Artichoke Cottage Cheese Bowls

![Blistered Tomato and Artichoke Cottage Cheese Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\blistered_tomato_and_artichoke_cottage_cheese_bowls.png)

A satisfying working lunch needs to be quick to assemble but substantial enough to carry you through the afternoon. This bowl plays the warm, tangy pop of blistered vegetables against a cool, creamy bed of savory cottage cheese.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups (350 g) **Low-sodium low-fat cottage cheese (2% milkfat)**
- 1 cup (200 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 7 oz (200 g) **Canned no-salt-added artichoke hearts**, rinsed, drained, and roughly chopped
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Balsamic vinegar**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 3 tbsp (30 g) **Shelled unsalted pistachios**, roughly chopped
- 1/4 cup (10 g) **Fresh basil**, torn
- 2 medium (100 g) **Whole-wheat pitas**, warmed and cut into wedges

## Instructions
1. Toss the tomatoes, chopped artichokes, and garlic powder together in a medium bowl.
2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
3. Add the tomato and artichoke mixture to the skillet and cook for 4 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes begin to burst and the artichokes brown.
4. Remove the skillet from the heat and stir in the balsamic vinegar and black pepper, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
5. Divide the cold cottage cheese evenly between shallow bowls.
6. Spoon the warm tomato and artichoke mixture directly over the cottage cheese.
7. Top with the chopped pistachios and fresh basil, and serve immediately with the warm whole-wheat pita wedges.

## Variation
Swap the chopped pistachios for toasted slivered almonds or walnuts.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat the tomato mixture before assembling.
