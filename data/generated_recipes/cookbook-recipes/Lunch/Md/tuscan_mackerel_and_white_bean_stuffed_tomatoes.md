# Tuscan Mackerel and White Bean Stuffed Tomatoes

![Tuscan Mackerel and White Bean Stuffed Tomatoes](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\tuscan_mackerel_and_white_bean_stuffed_tomatoes.png)

This totally zero-cook assembly is ready in minutes with minimal chopping. Flaky mackerel and creamy white beans make a satisfying filling for fresh tomatoes.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 15 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 large (400 g) **Beefsteak tomatoes**
- 8 oz (225 g) **No-salt-added canned skinless boneless mackerel in water**, drained
- 3/4 cup (130 g) **No-salt-added canned cannellini beans**, rinsed and drained
- 2 tbsp (18 g) **Kalamata olives**, pitted and finely chopped
- 1/4 cup (15 g) **Fresh flat-leaf parsley**, chopped
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Whole-wheat pita bread**

## Instructions
1. Slice the top off each tomato and scoop out the seeds and core with a spoon, leaving a hollow shell. Set aside.
2. Place the beans in a medium bowl and mash lightly with a fork until mostly broken down but still textured.
3. Add the drained mackerel, olives, parsley, lemon juice, olive oil, and black pepper to the mashed beans. Fold gently to combine without completely turning the fish to paste.
4. Spoon the mackerel mixture evenly into the hollowed tomatoes, pressing lightly to fill the cavities.
5. Cut the whole-wheat pita bread into wedges and serve alongside the stuffed tomatoes.

## Variation
Swap the cannellini beans for no-salt-added canned chickpeas.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
