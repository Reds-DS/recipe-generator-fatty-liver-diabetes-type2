# Velvety Butternut Squash and White Bean Soup

![Velvety Butternut Squash and White Bean Soup](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Lunch\IMG\velvety_butternut_squash_and_white_bean_soup.png)

Skipping the peeling and chopping by using frozen butternut squash makes this hearty soup fast to prepare. Blended white beans create a creamy texture, finished with a dollop of Greek yogurt and crunchy pumpkin seeds.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 15–19 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Frozen diced butternut squash**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Onion powder**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Dried sage**
- 2 cups (480 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 7 oz (200 g) **No-salt-added canned Great Northern beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Salt**
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 4 oz (113 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 1.5 oz (40 g) **Unsalted roasted pumpkin seeds (pepitas)**

## Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a medium pot over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes, checking at 1 minute, until shimmering.
2. Add the frozen butternut squash, garlic powder, onion powder, and dried sage. Sauté for 4 to 5 minutes, checking at 4 minutes, until the squash softens.
3. Pour in the vegetable broth and stir in the rinsed Great Northern beans, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce the heat to medium-low. Simmer for 10 to 12 minutes, checking at 10 minutes, until the squash is tender.
4. Remove the pot from the heat. Purée the soup directly in the pot using an immersion blender until completely smooth.
5. Ladle the soup into bowls. Dollop the Greek yogurt into the center of each bowl and sprinkle with the pumpkin seeds before serving.

## Variation
Swap the dried sage for an equal amount of dried thyme.

## Storage
Keeps 3-4 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
