# Parchment-Baked Lemon Cod with Zucchini and Couscous

![Parchment-Baked Lemon Cod with Zucchini and Couscous](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\parchment-baked_lemon_cod_with_zucchini_and_couscous.png)

Cooking in folded parchment paper locks in moisture and infuses the fish with bright Mediterranean flavors. Whole wheat couscous steams easily on the counter to complete this simple meal.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 12–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 12 oz (340 g) **Skinless cod fillets**, patted dry
- 12 oz (340 g) **Zucchini**, diced
- 8 oz (225 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 1 oz (30 g) **Kalamata olives, low-sodium**, rinsed, drained, and halved
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**
- 1 medium **Fresh lemon**, halved
- 1/2 cup (85 g) **Whole wheat couscous, dry**
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **Boiling water**

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C. Tear off large squares of parchment paper.
2. Toss the zucchini, cherry tomatoes, olives, extra-virgin olive oil, oregano, black pepper, and salt in a bowl.
3. Divide the vegetable mixture onto the parchment sheets and top with the cod fillets. Squeeze lemon juice over the fish, then fold the parchment edges tightly to seal into packets.
4. Place the packets on a baking sheet and bake for 12 to 15 minutes. Check the fish at 12 minutes to ensure it is opaque and flakes easily.
5. Place the whole wheat couscous in a heatproof bowl. Pour the boiling water over the grains, cover tightly, and let sit for 5 minutes.
6. Fluff the couscous with a fork. Cut the remaining lemon into wedges.
7. Divide the couscous onto plates. Open the hot packets carefully, slide the cod and vegetables over the grains, and serve with the lemon wedges.

## Variation
Swap the cod fillets for equal amounts of peeled shrimp.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently in the oven.
