# Skillet Chicken and Green Pea Curry Bowls

![Skillet Chicken and Green Pea Curry Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\skillet_chicken_and_green_pea_curry_bowls.png)

Perfect for a busy weeknight dinner, this spiced skillet dish swaps traditional heavy cream for plain Greek yogurt to build a rich, comforting sauce.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8 oz (227 g) **Boneless, skinless chicken breast tenders**, cut into bite-sized chunks
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 1 tbsp (6 g) **Yellow curry powder**
- 1 cup (240 g) **No-salt-added canned crushed tomatoes**
- 1 cup (134 g) **Frozen green peas**
- 1 1/2 cups (240 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked brown rice pouch**
- 1/3 cup (75 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 1/4 cup (4 g) **Fresh cilantro**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Warm the extra-virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add the chicken chunks and cook for 4 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned on the outside. Check at 4 minutes.
3. Lower the heat to medium. Stir in the minced garlic and yellow curry powder, cooking for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant. Check at 1 minute.
4. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and frozen green peas, stirring to combine. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 5 to 7 minutes until the chicken is completely cooked through and the sauce thickens. Check at 5 minutes.
5. Warm the brown rice pouch in the microwave according to the package directions while the curry simmers.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat. Stir the Greek yogurt into the curry until smoothly incorporated into a creamy sauce.
7. Divide the warm brown rice between two shallow bowls. Spoon the chicken curry over the top and garnish with fresh chopped cilantro.

## Variation
Swap the frozen green peas for small frozen broccoli florets.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently to maintain sauce texture.
