# Pan-Seared Trout with Lemon-Dill Quinoa and Peas

![Pan-Seared Trout with Lemon-Dill Quinoa and Peas](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\pan-seared_trout_with_lemon-dill_quinoa_and_peas.png)

Instead of boiling a separate pot for grains, this technique tosses a ready-to-eat quinoa pouch directly into the skillet to absorb the savory pan juices left by the trout. It perfectly bridges the gap between whole-food nutrition and a weeknight pantry shortcut.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 7–11 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 9 oz (255 g) **Rainbow trout fillets**, patted dry
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 8.5 oz (240 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa pouch**
- 1 1/2 cups (200 g) **Frozen green peas**
- 3 oz (85 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Lemon juice**, freshly squeezed
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Dijon mustard**
- 1/4 cup (4 g) **Fresh dill**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Pat the trout dry with a paper towel and season the flesh evenly with the black pepper.
2. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place the trout skin-side down and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until the skin is crisp. Check at 3 minutes.
3. Carefully flip the trout and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily. Transfer the trout to a plate and cover loosely to keep warm.
4. Reduce the skillet heat to medium. Add the ready-to-eat quinoa and frozen peas to the remaining pan juices. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring frequently until heated through. Check at 2 minutes.
5. Add the baby spinach to the skillet and toss for 1 to 2 minutes, until just wilted. Remove the skillet from the heat.
6. Stir the lemon juice, Dijon mustard, and fresh dill directly into the warm quinoa mixture, mixing well to combine the flavors.
7. Divide the warm quinoa and pea salad between two plates and top each with a pan-seared trout fillet.

## Variation
Swap the trout fillets for boneless, skinless salmon or tilapia.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently in the microwave.
