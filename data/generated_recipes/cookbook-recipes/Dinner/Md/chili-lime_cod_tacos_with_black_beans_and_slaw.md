# Chili-Lime Cod Tacos with Black Beans and Slaw

![Chili-Lime Cod Tacos with Black Beans and Slaw](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\chili-lime_cod_tacos_with_black_beans_and_slaw.png)

Zesty, bright, and deeply savory, these pan-seared cod tacos offer the crisp textures of taco night without deep-frying. The black beans build a high-fiber carbohydrate base that helps provide steady energy.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–13 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups **Pre-shredded red cabbage**
- 1/4 cup **Fresh cilantro**, chopped
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lime juice**, divided
- 1/2 medium **Avocado**, diced
- 10 oz (285 g) **Untreated fresh or frozen cod fillets**, thawed if frozen
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Chili powder**
- 1/8 tsp (0.75 g) **Salt**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 cup (260 g) **No-salt-added canned black beans**, rinsed and drained
- 4 tortillas **Corn tortillas**

## Instructions
1. Toss the cabbage, cilantro, half the lime juice, and the avocado in a medium bowl. Mash the avocado into the greens to dress the slaw, then set aside.
2. Pat the cod dry with a paper towel. Season on all sides with the chili powder and salt.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the cod and cook for 3 to 4 minutes per side, flipping halfway, until golden and opaque.
4. Transfer the cod to a clean plate and flake it into bite-sized pieces with a fork. Drizzle the remaining lime juice over the flaked fish.
5. Reduce the stovetop heat to medium and add the rinsed black beans to the same skillet. Stir frequently for 2 to 3 minutes until warmed through, then transfer to a serving bowl.
6. Wipe out the skillet and place it over medium-high heat. Warm the corn tortillas for 30 seconds per side, flipping halfway, until soft and pliable.
7. Divide the warm tortillas between two plates and fill with the flaked cod. Top with the avocado slaw and serve the warm black beans alongside.

## Variation
Swap the cod fillets for untreated, peeled medium shrimp.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep well.
