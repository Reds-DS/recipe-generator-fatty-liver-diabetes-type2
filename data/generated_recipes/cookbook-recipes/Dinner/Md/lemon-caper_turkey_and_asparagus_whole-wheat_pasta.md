# Lemon-Caper Turkey and Asparagus Whole-Wheat Pasta

![Lemon-Caper Turkey and Asparagus Whole-Wheat Pasta](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\lemon-caper_turkey_and_asparagus_whole-wheat_pasta.png)

This fast dinner requires minimal prep and uses a simple skillet technique. Quick-cooking turkey cutlets and whole-wheat angel hair pasta are tossed with asparagus in a bright lemon-caper reduction.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 4 oz (115 g) **Whole-wheat angel hair pasta**
- 9 oz (250 g) **Fresh asparagus**, woody ends trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Turkey breast cutlets**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Capers**, rinsed and drained
- 2 tbsp (5 g) **Fresh flat-leaf parsley**, roughly chopped

## Instructions
1. Bring water to a boil in a medium pot over high heat. Add the pasta and cook for 3 to 5 minutes.
2. Stir the asparagus into the boiling water during the final 2 minutes of the cooking time. Drain the pasta and asparagus and set aside.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the turkey cutlets for 2 to 3 minutes per side, flipping at the halfway point. Transfer the turkey to a plate.
4. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the garlic and cook for 30 to 60 seconds, stirring constantly.
5. Pour the broth, lemon juice, and capers into the skillet. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes, scraping the pan to release any browned bits.
6. Return the turkey, pasta, and asparagus to the skillet. Toss for 1 to 2 minutes to coat the ingredients in the sauce.
7. Remove the skillet from the heat. Divide the meal between plates and garnish with the parsley.

## Variation
Swap the turkey breast cutlets for thinly sliced chicken breast.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently in a covered skillet.
