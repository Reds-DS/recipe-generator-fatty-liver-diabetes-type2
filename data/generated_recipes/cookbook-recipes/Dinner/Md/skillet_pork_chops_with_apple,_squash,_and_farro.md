# Skillet Pork Chops with Apple, Squash, and Farro

![Skillet Pork Chops with Apple, Squash, and Farro](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\skillet_pork_chops_with_apple,_squash,_and_farro.png)

Providing fiber and lean protein, this one-pan dinner helps keep your energy steady. Crisp apple naturally balances the savory mustard sauce without requiring added sugar.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 11–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 10 oz (283 g) **Boneless pork loin chops**, patted dry
- 1/8 tsp (1 g) **Salt**
- 1/2 tsp (2 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1.5 cups (210 g) **Frozen butternut squash chunks**
- 1 medium (150 g) **Crisp apple (such as Fuji or Gala)**, cored and diced
- 1 tbsp (2 g) **Fresh sage**, chopped
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Apple cider vinegar**
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Dijon mustard**
- 5.5 oz (156 g) **Unseasoned ready-to-eat cooked farro**, from a pouch
- 2 cups (60 g) **Fresh baby spinach**

## Instructions
1. Season the pork chops on both sides with salt and garlic powder.
2. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the pork chops and sear for 4 to 5 minutes per side, flipping halfway, until golden brown. Transfer the pork to a plate and cover to keep warm.
3. Add the butternut squash and diced apple to the same skillet. Cook over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring, until the squash softens and lightly browns.
4. Stir in the fresh sage, low-sodium chicken broth, apple cider vinegar, and Dijon mustard, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Simmer the liquid for 1 to 2 minutes until it thickens.
5. Stir in the cooked farro and baby spinach. Toss for 1 to 2 minutes until the spinach wilts and the grains are hot.
6. Return the warm pork chops and any resting juices to the skillet. Heat for 1 minute before dividing everything between two plates.

## Variation
Swap the butternut squash for frozen cubed sweet potatoes.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; reheat gently in a covered skillet.
