# Moroccan Chicken and Chickpea Skillet with Quinoa

![Moroccan Chicken and Chickpea Skillet with Quinoa](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\moroccan_chicken_and_chickpea_skillet_with_quinoa.png)

Cinnamon is typically reserved for baking, but in North African cooking, it brings a warm, savory depth that transforms a simple pan sauce. Built with pre-cooked quinoa and tender chicken, this braise delivers complex flavors without demanding hours on the stove.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–14 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8 oz (220 g) **Chicken tenderloins**, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Ground cinnamon**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Kosher salt**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 1 cup (240 g) **No-salt-added diced tomatoes, with juice**
- 1/2 cup (130 g) **No-salt-added canned chickpeas**, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup (185 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa pouch**
- 3 cups (90 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**

## Instructions
1. Toss the chicken in a medium bowl with the ground cumin, smoked paprika, ground cinnamon, and salt until evenly coated.
2. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned on the bottom, 3 to 4 minutes.
3. Flip the chicken and add the minced garlic to the skillet. Stir and cook for 1 to 2 minutes.
4. Pour the diced tomatoes with their juices, rinsed chickpeas, and cooked quinoa into the skillet. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer.
5. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover, and cook until the chicken is completely opaque and the quinoa is warmed, 5 to 6 minutes.
6. Remove the lid and stir in the baby spinach. Cook until the spinach wilts, 1 to 2 minutes.
7. Remove the skillet from the heat, stir in the fresh lemon juice, and divide between two plates.

## Variation
Swap the chicken for turkey breast or boneless pork loin.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
