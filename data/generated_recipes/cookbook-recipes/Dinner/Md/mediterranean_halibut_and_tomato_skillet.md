# Mediterranean Halibut and Tomato Skillet

![Mediterranean Halibut and Tomato Skillet](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\mediterranean_halibut_and_tomato_skillet.png)

This satisfying skillet meal relies on blistered cherry tomatoes to build a flavorful pan sauce. The savory broth simmers the lean halibut and perfectly seasons the whole-wheat couscous.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–14 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup (120 g) **Dried whole-wheat couscous**
- 3/4 cup (180 ml) **Boiling water**
- 10 oz (280 g) **Fresh halibut fillets**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 9 oz (250 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 3 cloves (9 g) **Fresh garlic**, minced
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 4 oz (120 g) **Baby spinach**
- 1/4 cup (30 g) **Low-sodium Kalamata olives**, pitted, rinsed, drained and halved
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**

## Instructions
1. Place the couscous in a heatproof bowl. Pour in the boiling water, cover tightly, let sit for 5 minutes to hydrate, and fluff with a fork.
2. Season the halibut with the dried oregano, black pepper, and salt.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the halibut for 3 to 4 minutes on one side until golden, flip the fish, and transfer to a plate.
4. Reduce the heat to medium and add the cherry tomatoes and garlic to the skillet. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes begin to blister and burst.
5. Pour in the vegetable broth, scraping up any browned bits from the pan. Stir in the spinach and olives, cooking for 1 to 2 minutes until the spinach wilts.
6. Return the halibut to the skillet. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes, spooning the tomato sauce over the fish, until the fish is completely opaque and flakes easily.
7. Remove the skillet from the heat and pour in the lemon juice. Serve the halibut and blistered tomato sauce over the steamed couscous.

## Variation
Swap the halibut for an equal amount of peeled shrimp.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
