# Smoky Scallop, Corn, and Zucchini Quinoa Skillet

![Smoky Scallop, Corn, and Zucchini Quinoa Skillet](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\smoky_scallop,_corn,_and_zucchini_quinoa_skillet.png)

Delivering lean protein and steady energy, this quick stovetop meal helps keep you full on busy weeknights. Scallops cook in minutes alongside high-fiber vegetables for a vibrant, all-in-one skillet.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 8–11 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 12 oz (340 g) **Sea scallops, untreated / no salt added**, side muscle removed
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**, divided
- 1 medium (200 g) **Zucchini**, diced
- 1 medium (150 g) **Red bell pepper**, diced
- 1 cup (145 g) **Frozen sweet corn**
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa pouch**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Smoked paprika**, divided
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**, divided
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lime juice**
- 1/4 cup (8 g) **Fresh cilantro**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Pat the scallops dry with a paper towel. Season them with half the smoked paprika, half the garlic powder, and the black pepper.
2. Heat half the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the scallops and sear for 4 to 6 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown on the outside and mostly opaque in the center. Transfer the scallops to a plate and set aside.
3. Add the remaining olive oil, diced zucchini, diced red bell pepper, and frozen sweet corn to the same skillet. Sauté over medium-high heat for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables begin to soften and char.
4. Crumble the quinoa into the skillet with the vegetables. Stir in the remaining smoked paprika, remaining garlic powder, and the water to lift any browned bits from the pan. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until heated through.
5. Return the scallops and any resting juices to the skillet. Drizzle the lime juice over the top and sprinkle with the chopped cilantro.
6. Toss the mixture to combine, remove the skillet from the heat, and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the sea scallops for untreated large peeled shrimp.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; leftover scallops do not reheat well.
