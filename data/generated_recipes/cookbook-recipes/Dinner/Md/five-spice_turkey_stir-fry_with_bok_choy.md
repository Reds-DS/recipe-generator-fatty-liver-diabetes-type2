# Five-Spice Turkey Stir-Fry with Bok Choy

![Five-Spice Turkey Stir-Fry with Bok Choy](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\five-spice_turkey_stir-fry_with_bok_choy.png)

This fast, single-skillet dinner relies on quick-cooking turkey breast and a convenient brown rice pouch to keep weeknight preparation simple. Warm Chinese five-spice powder and fresh ginger deliver savory flavor without the heavy sugar of standard sauces.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 6–9 min
- **Prep time:** 12 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8 oz (225 g) **Turkey breast cutlets**, sliced into thin strips
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Chinese five-spice powder**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Fresh ginger**, peeled and minced
- 8 oz (225 g) **Sugar snap peas**, trimmed
- 8 oz (225 g) **Baby bok choy**, chopped
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Unseasoned rice vinegar**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Low-sodium soy sauce**
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked brown rice pouch**
- 1 tbsp (9 g) **Toasted sesame seeds**

## Instructions
1. Toss the turkey strips in a bowl with the Chinese five-spice powder until evenly coated.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned turkey and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring frequently, until lightly browned.
3. Add the ginger, sugar snap peas, and bok choy to the skillet. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring constantly, until the vegetables turn bright green.
4. Pour the chicken broth, rice vinegar, and low-sodium soy sauce into the pan. Simmer for 1 to 2 minutes, tossing well, until the turkey is cooked through and the sauce lightly coats the ingredients.
5. Heat the brown rice pouch in the microwave for 1 to 2 minutes until steaming hot.
6. Divide the warm brown rice between two plates. Top evenly with the turkey and vegetable stir-fry, then garnish with the toasted sesame seeds.

## Variation
Swap the turkey breast for lean pork tenderloin strips.

## Storage
Keeps 2 to 3 days refrigerated; reheat on the stovetop.
