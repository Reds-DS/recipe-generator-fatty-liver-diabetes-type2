# Spiced Red Lentil and Spinach Stew

![Spiced Red Lentil and Spinach Stew](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\spiced_red_lentil_and_spinach_stew.png)

This simple one-pot stew relies on quick-cooking dry red lentils and pre-chopped onion for a fast weeknight dinner. Nonfat Greek yogurt adds creamy richness and protein while skipping the saturated fat of coconut milk.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 18–23 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Pre-chopped yellow onion**
- 2 cloves **Minced garlic**, minced
- 1 tbsp (6 g) **Curry powder**
- 4.2 oz (120 g) **Dry red lentils**, rinsed
- 8.5 oz (240 g) **No-salt-added canned diced tomatoes**, with juices
- 2 cups (480 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 10.6 oz (300 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 0.4 oz (10 g) **Fresh cilantro**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a medium pot over medium heat until shimmering. Add the yellow onion and minced garlic, and cook until softened, about 3 to 4 minutes.
2. Stir in the curry powder and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Pour in the dry red lentils, diced tomatoes with their juices, and vegetable broth. Increase the heat to medium-high and bring to a gentle boil.
4. Reduce the heat to low, cover the pot tightly, and simmer until the lentils are tender and the liquid is absorbed. Check at 12 minutes, cooking for 12 to 15 minutes total.
5. Remove the pot from the heat. Fold in the baby spinach, stirring until the residual heat wilts the leaves, then let the stew sit for 2 minutes to cool.
6. Fold in the Greek yogurt and chopped cilantro until the stew becomes creamy and uniform in color. Plate and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the baby spinach for chopped kale or Swiss chard.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently over low heat without boiling.
