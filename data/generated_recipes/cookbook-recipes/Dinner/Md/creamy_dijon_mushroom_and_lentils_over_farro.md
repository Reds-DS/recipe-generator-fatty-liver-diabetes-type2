# Creamy Dijon Mushroom and Lentils Over Farro

![Creamy Dijon Mushroom and Lentils Over Farro](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\creamy_dijon_mushroom_and_lentils_over_farro.png)

Trading heavy cream and beef for nonfat Greek yogurt and lentils turns a classic stroganoff into a satisfying weekday dinner. This earthy dish delivers a comforting tang alongside plenty of plant-based protein and fiber.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 8–11 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 8 oz (225 g) **Cremini mushrooms**, sliced
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Fresh thyme**, chopped
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Dijon mustard**
- 1 cup (200 g) **No-salt-added canned brown lentils**, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup (225 g) **Plain nonfat Greek yogurt**, at room temperature
- 1 cup (140 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked farro pouch**
- 1 oz (28 g) **Chopped walnuts**

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deeply browned and the moisture evaporates.
3. Stir in the garlic and thyme, and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Pour in the broth and mustard, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan, and bring to a simmer.
5. Reduce the heat to medium-low, stir in the lentils, and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes. Remove the skillet from the heat.
6. Warm the farro in the microwave according to the pouch directions. Let the lentil skillet cool off the heat, then stir in the yogurt until smoothly combined so it does not curdle.
7. Divide the warm farro between serving bowls, top with the mushroom and lentil mixture, and garnish with the walnuts.

## Variation
Swap the farro for a ready-to-eat brown rice pouch.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently over low heat.
