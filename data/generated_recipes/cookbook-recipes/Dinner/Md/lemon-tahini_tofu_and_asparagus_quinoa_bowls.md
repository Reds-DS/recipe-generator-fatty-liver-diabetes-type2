# Lemon-Tahini Tofu and Asparagus Quinoa Bowls

![Lemon-Tahini Tofu and Asparagus Quinoa Bowls](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\lemon-tahini_tofu_and_asparagus_quinoa_bowls.png)

This fast, single-skillet dinner relies on a ready-to-eat quinoa pouch and quick-searing extra-firm tofu to get food on the table with minimal effort. Creamy Greek yogurt stretches the rich tahini dressing to deliver classic Mediterranean flavor.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–14 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup (100 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 2 tsp (10 g) **Tahini**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**, divided
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Extra-firm tofu**, pressed and cubed
- 7 oz (200 g) **Fresh asparagus**, tough ends trimmed, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 3/4 cup (150 g) **Low-sodium canned chickpeas**, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup (150 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 7 oz (200 g) **Plain cooked quinoa**, from a ready-to-eat pouch
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Garlic powder**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the yogurt, tahini, lemon juice, half of the oregano, and warm water in a small bowl until smooth, then set aside.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering, then add the cubed tofu in a single layer.
3. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes, turning halfway through, until the tofu is golden brown on most sides, then push the tofu to one side of the skillet.
4. Add the asparagus and water to the empty side of the pan and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, tossing often, until tender-crisp and the water evaporates.
5. Stir the chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, quinoa, remaining oregano, and garlic powder into the skillet with the tofu and asparagus.
6. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, tossing everything together constantly, until the quinoa and tomatoes are fully heated through.
7. Divide the skillet mixture between plates and drizzle with the creamy dressing to serve.

## Variation
Swap the asparagus for roughly chopped green beans or zucchini.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; store the dressing separately and reheat.
