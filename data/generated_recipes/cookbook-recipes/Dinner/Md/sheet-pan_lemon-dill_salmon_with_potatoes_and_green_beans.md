# Sheet-Pan Lemon-Dill Salmon with Potatoes and Green Beans

![Sheet-Pan Lemon-Dill Salmon with Potatoes and Green Beans](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\sheet-pan_lemon-dill_salmon_with_potatoes_and_green_beans.png)

This single-pan meal brings together salmon, skin-on potatoes, and crisp greens for a filling combination of lean protein and dietary fiber. A mustard and herb coating keeps the fish flavorful without relying on heavy sauces.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 20–22 min
- **Prep time:** 8 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 10 oz (285 g) **Salmon**, fillets, untreated
- 14 oz (400 g) **Baby potatoes**, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 8 oz (225 g) **Fresh green beans**, trimmed
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Whole grain mustard**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Lemon juice**, freshly squeezed
- 1 tbsp (3 g) **Fresh dill**, chopped
- 1 tbsp (8 g) **Sliced almonds**

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 425°F / 220°C for 5 minutes.
2. Toss the cubed potatoes directly on a baking sheet with the olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
3. Spread the potatoes into an even layer and roast for 10 minutes until they begin to soften.
4. Mix the whole grain mustard, lemon juice, and fresh dill in a small bowl.
5. Remove the baking sheet from the oven, push the potatoes to one side, and place the green beans in the middle.
6. Place the salmon fillets on the remaining open third of the pan and spoon the mustard mixture evenly over the fish.
7. Return the pan to the oven and roast for 10 to 12 minutes, checking at 10 minutes, until the salmon is opaque and flakes easily with a fork. Scatter the almonds over the top before serving.

## Variation
Swap the fresh green beans for trimmed asparagus spears.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently in the oven.
