# Beef and Lentil Meatballs with Zucchini and Quinoa

![Beef and Lentil Meatballs with Zucchini and Quinoa](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\beef_and_lentil_meatballs_with_zucchini_and_quinoa.png)

Tender, herbaceous meatballs simmer in a rich tomato base for a deeply savory and comforting meal. Mashing brown lentils directly into the lean beef mixture lowers the saturated fat while keeping the texture soft and boosting the fiber.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 14–18 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (100 g) **No-salt-added canned brown lentils**, rinsed and drained
- 8 oz (225 g) **Extra-lean ground beef (96% lean)**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 medium (200 g) **Zucchini**, diced
- 1 cup (240 g) **No-salt-added crushed tomatoes**
- 1 cup (200 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa pouch**
- 1/4 cup (15 g) **Fresh parsley**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Place the drained lentils in a mixing bowl and mash them roughly with a fork.
2. Add the ground beef, garlic powder, dried oregano, salt, and half the fresh parsley to the bowl. Mix gently with your hands to combine, then roll the mixture into evenly sized meatballs.
3. Warm the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Add the meatballs and cook for 4 to 5 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned on all sides.
4. Add the diced zucchini to the skillet and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the zucchini begins to soften.
5. Pour the crushed tomatoes over the meatballs and zucchini. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.
6. Cover the skillet, reduce the heat to medium-low, and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes. Check the meatballs at 8 minutes to ensure they are no longer pink in the center.
7. Warm the quinoa pouch in the microwave while the skillet finishes simmering. Divide the quinoa between plates, top with the meatballs and sauce, and garnish with the remaining fresh parsley.

## Variation
Swap the diced zucchini for chopped yellow bell pepper.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently in the microwave.
