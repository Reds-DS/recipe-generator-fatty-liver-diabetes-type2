# Smoky Chicken and Sweet Potato Skillet Hash

![Smoky Chicken and Sweet Potato Skillet Hash](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\smoky_chicken_and_sweet_potato_skillet_hash.png)

A covered-skillet steaming trick makes this meal fast to prepare. Tender sweet potatoes, smoky chicken thighs, and hearty kale anchor a satisfying, high-fiber hash.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 14–20 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 12 oz (340 g) **Boneless, skinless chicken thighs**, diced into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 12 oz (340 g) **Sweet potatoes**, peeled and diced small
- 2.5 oz (70 g) **Red onion**, diced
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Bagged chopped kale**, tough stems removed
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Apple cider vinegar**
- 2 tbsp (18 g) **Raw pumpkin seeds (pepitas)**

## Instructions
1. Toss the chicken with the smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper in a medium bowl.
2. Heat the oil in a large skillet with a tight-fitting lid over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned chicken and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until browned on the outside, then transfer it to a plate.
3. Add the sweet potatoes and red onion to the same skillet. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring to pick up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
4. Pour in the chicken broth and immediately cover the skillet. Reduce the heat to medium-low and steam for 6 to 8 minutes, checking at 6 minutes, until the sweet potatoes are tender.
5. Uncover the skillet and stir in the kale. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the leaves begin to wilt.
6. Return the chicken and any resting juices to the skillet. Drizzle with the apple cider vinegar and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the chicken is completely cooked through and the moisture has mostly evaporated.
7. Divide the hash between plates and top with the pumpkin seeds before serving.

## Variation
Swap the chopped kale for fresh spinach or Swiss chard.

## Storage
Keeps 2 to 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a warm skillet.
