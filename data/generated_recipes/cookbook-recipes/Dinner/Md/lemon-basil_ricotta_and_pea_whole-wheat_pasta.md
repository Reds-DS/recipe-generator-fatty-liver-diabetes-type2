# Lemon-Basil Ricotta and Pea Whole-Wheat Pasta

![Lemon-Basil Ricotta and Pea Whole-Wheat Pasta](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\lemon-basil_ricotta_and_pea_whole-wheat_pasta.png)

With just one pot and a few simple steps, this stovetop dinner is effortless to prepare. It combines whole-wheat pasta and green peas with a protein-rich blend of part-skim ricotta and Greek yogurt.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 9–11 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 3 oz (85 g) **Whole-wheat penne or rotini**
- 1.5 cups (200 g) **Frozen green peas**
- 3.5 oz (100 g) **Part-skim ricotta cheese**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Plain non-fat Greek yogurt**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Lemon zest**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1/2 cup (12 g) **Fresh basil**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil over high heat.
2. Add the pasta and cook for 9 to 11 minutes. Stir in the frozen peas for the final 2 minutes of cooking.
3. Reserve some of the cooking water, then drain the pasta and peas. Return the mixture to the warm pot off the heat.
4. Add the ricotta, Greek yogurt, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, and black pepper directly to the warm pot.
5. Toss the mixture vigorously, splashing in the reserved cooking water as needed, until a creamy sauce forms and coats the pasta.
6. Fold in the fresh basil and serve warm.

## Variation
Swap the frozen green peas for frozen shelled edamame.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
