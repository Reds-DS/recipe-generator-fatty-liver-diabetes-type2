# Italian White Bean Stuffed Portobellos with Quinoa

![Italian White Bean Stuffed Portobellos with Quinoa](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\italian_white_bean_stuffed_portobellos_with_quinoa.png)

Few ingredients and minimal prep make this plant-based dinner a fast weeknight staple. Meaty portobello caps serve as edible bowls for a savory bean and spinach filling, paired with a convenient quinoa pouch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 15–18 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 4 caps (340 g) **Large portobello mushroom caps**, wiped clean and stems removed
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 9 oz (250 g) **No-salt-added canned cannellini beans**, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups (60 g) **Fresh baby spinach**, roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup (125 g) **No-added-sugar marinara sauce**
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 2.5 oz (70 g) **Shredded part-skim mozzarella cheese**
- 1/2 cup (90 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa pouch**

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Brush the outsides of the portobello caps with the extra-virgin olive oil and place them gill-side up on the prepared baking sheet.
3. Mash half of the cannellini beans in a medium bowl using a fork. Stir in the remaining whole beans, spinach, marinara sauce, and garlic powder until combined.
4. Divide the bean and spinach mixture among the mushroom caps. Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella over the tops.
5. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes until the mushrooms are tender and the cheese is melted and bubbling.
6. Warm the quinoa in the microwave according to the pouch instructions while the mushrooms bake.
7. Serve the stuffed portobellos alongside the warm quinoa.

## Variation
Swap the baby spinach for finely chopped fresh kale.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in the oven for 10 minutes.
