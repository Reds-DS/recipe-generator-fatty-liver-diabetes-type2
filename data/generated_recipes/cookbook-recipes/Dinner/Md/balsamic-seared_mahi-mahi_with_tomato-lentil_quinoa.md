# Balsamic-Seared Mahi-Mahi with Tomato-Lentil Quinoa

![Balsamic-Seared Mahi-Mahi with Tomato-Lentil Quinoa](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\balsamic-seared_mahi-mahi_with_tomato-lentil_quinoa.png)

Tangy balsamic vinegar creates a rich glaze over flaky seared fish and sweet blistered cherry tomatoes. Using a ready-to-eat quinoa pouch and canned lentils builds a high-fiber base without the wait.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 11–16 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 9 oz (255 g) **Mahi-Mahi fillets**, thawed if frozen, patted dry
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 1/2 cups (225 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, whole
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Balsamic vinegar**
- 1 cup (200 g) **No-salt-added canned brown lentils**, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup (185 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa**
- 2 cups (60 g) **Baby spinach**, loosely packed

## Instructions
1. Pat the fish fillets dry and season on both sides with the garlic powder, dried oregano, and black pepper.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the fish and sear for 3 to 4 minutes, flip at the halfway point, and cook for another 3 to 4 minutes until opaque, then transfer to a plate and cover.
3. Reduce the heat to medium and add the cherry tomatoes to the skillet. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes blister and begin to burst.
4. Pour the balsamic vinegar into the skillet and stir constantly to scrape up any browned bits from the bottom. Simmer for 1 to 2 minutes to reduce the liquid.
5. Stir the lentils and quinoa into the skillet to combine with the pan juices. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until completely heated through.
6. Fold in the baby spinach and toss gently for 1 to 2 minutes. Remove the skillet from the heat as soon as the leaves wilt.
7. Divide the warm lentil and quinoa mixture between shallow bowls. Top with a seared fillet and spoon the remaining pan juices over the fish.

## Variation
Swap the mahi-mahi fillets for firm white cod or tilapia.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
