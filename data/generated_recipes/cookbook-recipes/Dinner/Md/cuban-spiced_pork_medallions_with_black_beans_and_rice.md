# Cuban-Spiced Pork Medallions with Black Beans and Rice

![Cuban-Spiced Pork Medallions with Black Beans and Rice](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\cuban-spiced_pork_medallions_with_black_beans_and_rice.png)

Pairing lean pork with a base of black beans and brown rice delivers helpful fiber and steady-digesting carbohydrates to keep your energy stable. Slicing the tenderloin into thin medallions ensures it cooks rapidly without drying out.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 11–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 10 oz (283 g) **Pork tenderloin**, cut crosswise into 1/2-inch medallions
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 medium (55 g) **Red onion**, chopped
- 1 medium (119 g) **Green bell pepper**, chopped
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 3/4 cup (195 g) **No-salt-added canned black beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup (140 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked brown rice**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Fresh lime juice**

## Instructions
1. Toss the pork medallions with the cumin, oregano, and black pepper in a bowl to coat evenly.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the pork in a single layer and cook for 4 to 6 minutes, flipping halfway, until the meat is browned, then transfer to a plate.
3. Reduce the heat to medium and add the red onion and green bell pepper to the skillet. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables soften.
4. Stir the minced garlic into the skillet and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add the rinsed black beans, brown rice, and water to the pan. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring constantly, until the mixture is steaming and heated through.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat. Stir in the fresh lime juice, then return the pork medallions and resting juices to the pan, tossing gently to combine.
7. Divide the rice and vegetable mixture evenly between warm plates, and top with the pork medallions.

## Variation
Swap the pork tenderloin for boneless, skinless chicken breast pieces.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in the microwave until warmed through.
