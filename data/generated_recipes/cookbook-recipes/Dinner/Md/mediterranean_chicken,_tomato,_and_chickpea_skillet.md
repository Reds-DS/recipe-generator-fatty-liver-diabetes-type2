# Mediterranean Chicken, Tomato, and Chickpea Skillet

![Mediterranean Chicken, Tomato, and Chickpea Skillet](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\mediterranean_chicken,_tomato,_and_chickpea_skillet.png)

Simmering bite-sized chicken thighs with crushed tomatoes and chickpeas creates a hearty stew in a single pan. Serve this simple, comforting meal with warm whole-wheat pitas for dipping.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 14–18 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 10.5 oz (300 g) **Boneless, skinless chicken thighs**, chopped into bite-sized pieces
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Zucchini**, sliced into half-moons
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 10.5 oz (300 g) **No-salt-added canned diced tomatoes**, undrained
- 5.3 oz (150 g) **No-salt-added canned chickpeas**, rinsed and drained
- 1 oz (30 g) **Kalamata olives**, pitted and halved
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 2 small (66 g) **Small whole-wheat pitas**, warmed and halved

## Instructions
1. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add the chicken pieces and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until lightly browned on all sides.
3. Stir in the zucchini, garlic powder, and dried oregano, and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the zucchini begins to soften.
4. Pour in the diced tomatoes and chickpeas, stir well to combine, and bring to a rolling simmer.
5. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover the skillet, and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat, then stir in the kalamata olives and fresh lemon juice.
7. Serve the warm stew in bowls accompanied by the whole-wheat pitas.

## Variation
Swap the zucchini for diced eggplant and simmer until tender.

## Storage
Keeps 3-4 days refrigerated; reheat in a skillet over low heat.
