# Ginger-Soy Poached Haddock with Bok Choy and Brown Rice

![Ginger-Soy Poached Haddock with Bok Choy and Brown Rice](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\ginger-soy_poached_haddock_with_bok_choy_and_brown_rice.png)

This simple skillet meal provides lean protein and dietary fiber for a satisfying dinner. The savory ginger broth infuses the fish with flavor without adding excess sodium.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 6–8 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 1 tbsp (6 g) **Fresh ginger**, minced
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **No-salt-added vegetable broth**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Reduced-sodium soy sauce**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Unseasoned rice vinegar**
- 9 oz (255 g) **Haddock fillets**
- 1 cup (155 g) **Frozen shelled edamame**
- 8 oz (227 g) **Baby bok choy**, quartered
- 1.5 cups (290 g) **Ready-to-eat brown rice pouch**
- 2 medium (30 g) **Scallions**, thinly sliced

## Instructions
1. Chop the baby bok choy, mince the garlic and ginger, and slice the scallions.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, then add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Pour in the vegetable broth, reduced-sodium soy sauce, and rice vinegar, and bring the liquid to a simmer.
4. Nestle the haddock fillets and frozen edamame into the skillet, then cover and simmer for 2 minutes.
5. Scatter the baby bok choy over the fish and edamame. Cover the skillet and simmer for 3 to 5 minutes, checking at the 3-minute mark to ensure the fish is opaque and flakes easily and the greens are wilted.
6. Heat the brown rice in the microwave according to the package directions while the fish finishes cooking.
7. Divide the warm brown rice between serving bowls. Top with the haddock, edamame, and baby bok choy, spoon the broth over the bowls, and garnish with the scallions.

## Variation
Trade the haddock for cod or peeled large shrimp.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
