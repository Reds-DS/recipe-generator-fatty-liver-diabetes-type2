# Skillet White Bean and Zucchini Pizza Bake

![Skillet White Bean and Zucchini Pizza Bake](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\skillet_white_bean_and_zucchini_pizza_bake.png)

This savory one-pan meal delivers plenty of vegetables and plant-based protein for lasting fullness. Creamy navy beans replace a refined flour crust, providing a comforting, high-fiber base for classic pizza flavors.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 12–16 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Zucchini**, chopped
- 8 oz (225 g) **Cremini mushrooms**, sliced
- 1 tsp (3 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **No-salt-added navy beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup (240 g) **No-salt-added canned crushed tomatoes**
- 1/2 cup (56 g) **Part-skim mozzarella cheese**, shredded
- 2 tbsp (10 g) **Parmesan cheese**, grated

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C.
2. Heat the olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
3. Sauté the zucchini and mushrooms in the skillet until softened and lightly browned, 5 to 7 minutes.
4. Stir the garlic powder, dried oregano, navy beans, and crushed tomatoes into the skillet.
5. Reduce the heat to medium and simmer until the sauce thickens slightly, 3 to 4 minutes.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat and sprinkle the shredded mozzarella and grated Parmesan evenly over the top.
7. Transfer the skillet to the oven and bake until the cheese is melted and bubbling, 4 to 5 minutes.

## Variation
Swap the cremini mushrooms for diced green bell peppers.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in the microwave.
