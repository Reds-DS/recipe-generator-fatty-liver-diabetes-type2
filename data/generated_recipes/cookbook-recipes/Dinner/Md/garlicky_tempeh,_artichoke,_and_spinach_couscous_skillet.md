# Garlicky Tempeh, Artichoke, and Spinach Couscous Skillet

![Garlicky Tempeh, Artichoke, and Spinach Couscous Skillet](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\garlicky_tempeh,_artichoke,_and_spinach_couscous_skillet.png)

This hearty, plant-based dinner provides lasting energy and fullness by pairing protein-dense tempeh with fiber-rich whole-wheat couscous and artichokes.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 7–11 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Rest time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 8 oz (227 g) **Tempeh**, crumbled
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1/4 cup (15 g) **Sun-dried tomatoes**, chopped
- 1/2 can (120 g) **No-salt-added canned artichoke hearts**, rinsed, drained, and quartered
- 2/3 cup (160 ml) **Low-sodium vegetable broth**
- 2 cups (60 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 1/2 cup (85 g) **Whole-wheat couscous (dry)**
- 2 tbsp (15 g) **Feta cheese**, crumbled

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add crumbled tempeh and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown.
2. Reduce heat to medium. Stir in minced garlic, dried oregano, sun-dried tomatoes, and quartered artichoke hearts, and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the garlic is fragrant.
3. Pour in vegetable broth and bring to a simmer. Add baby spinach and stir for 1 to 2 minutes until wilted.
4. Stir in dry whole-wheat couscous, ensuring it is fully moistened by the broth and mixed with the vegetables.
5. Remove the skillet from the heat and cover tightly with a lid.
6. Uncover the skillet after resting and fluff the couscous mixture with a fork. Sprinkle crumbled feta cheese over the top before serving.

## Variation
Swap the baby spinach for fresh arugula or baby kale.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat briefly in the microwave.
