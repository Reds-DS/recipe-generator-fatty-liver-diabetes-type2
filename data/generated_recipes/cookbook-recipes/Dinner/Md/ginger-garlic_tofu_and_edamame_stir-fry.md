# Ginger-Garlic Tofu and Edamame Stir-Fry

![Ginger-Garlic Tofu and Edamame Stir-Fry](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\ginger-garlic_tofu_and_edamame_stir-fry.png)

Crisp broccoli, tender edamame, and golden tofu soak up a ginger-sesame sauce in this easy skillet meal. Combining two plant proteins provides a satisfying meatless dinner, and a microwave brown rice pouch serves as a quick base.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 9–13 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 12 oz (340 g) **Extra-firm tofu**, drained, pressed, and cubed
- 1 cup (150 g) **Frozen shelled edamame**
- 2 cups (180 g) **Broccoli florets**, chopped small
- 1 pouch (250 g) **Microwave brown rice**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Low-sodium soy sauce**
- 1.5 tbsp (22 ml) **Rice vinegar**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Water**
- 3 cloves (9 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 1 tbsp (6 g) **Fresh ginger**, minced
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Toasted sesame oil**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the low-sodium soy sauce, rice vinegar, water, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
2. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the tofu and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown on most sides.
4. Stir in the broccoli and frozen edamame, and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the broccoli turns bright green and the edamame thaws.
5. Pour the sauce into the skillet and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the liquid reduces and coats the tofu and vegetables.
6. Warm the brown rice in the microwave according to the package instructions.
7. Divide the warm brown rice between plates and top with the tofu and vegetable stir-fry.

## Variation
Swap the broccoli florets for chopped fresh bok choy.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; reheat in the microwave or a skillet.
