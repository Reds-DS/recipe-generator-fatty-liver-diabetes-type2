# Balsamic-Mustard Pork Medallions with Asparagus and Couscous

![Balsamic-Mustard Pork Medallions with Asparagus and Couscous](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\balsamic-mustard_pork_medallions_with_asparagus_and_couscous.png)

Building a pan sauce from tangy mustard and balsamic vinegar gives this skillet dinner deep flavor without excess saturated fat. Slicing a lean pork tenderloin into quick-cooking medallions keeps the meat tender and speeds up weeknight prep.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 12–16 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup (130 g) **Whole wheat couscous, dry**
- 3/4 cup (180 ml) **Water, boiling**
- 12 oz (340 g) **Pork tenderloin**, cut into 1-inch thick medallions
- 1/2 tsp (1.5 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/2 tsp (0.5 g) **Dried thyme**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 10 oz (280 g) **Asparagus**, tough ends snapped off, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Balsamic vinegar**
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Dijon mustard**

## Instructions
1. Combine the dry couscous and boiling water in a heatproof bowl. Cover tightly and set aside to steam.
2. Season the pork medallions evenly with the garlic powder, dried thyme, and black pepper.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned pork and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, flipping halfway, before transferring to a plate.
4. Place the asparagus pieces into the same skillet over medium heat. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, tossing occasionally, until tender-crisp, then transfer to the plate with the pork.
5. Lower the heat to medium-low and whisk the chicken broth, balsamic vinegar, and Dijon mustard into the skillet, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
6. Simmer the liquid for 2 to 3 minutes until slightly thickened into a pan sauce. Return the pork and asparagus to the skillet, turning them to coat evenly.
7. Fluff the steeped couscous with a fork and divide it between plates. Top with the glazed pork, asparagus, and any remaining pan sauce.

## Variation
Swap the asparagus for fresh green beans.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop.
