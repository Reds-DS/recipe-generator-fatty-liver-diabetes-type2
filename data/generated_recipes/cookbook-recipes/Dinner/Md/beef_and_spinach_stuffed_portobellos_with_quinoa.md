# Beef and Spinach Stuffed Portobellos with Quinoa

![Beef and Spinach Stuffed Portobellos with Quinoa](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\beef_and_spinach_stuffed_portobellos_with_quinoa.png)

Using large portobello caps as edible roasting vessels traps the savory juices of the filling, transforming an everyday ground beef skillet into a simple centerpiece. Serve these mushrooms alongside warm quinoa to soak up every drop of flavor.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 4 large caps (340 g) **Portobello mushroom caps**, wiped clean, stems removed
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**, divided
- 8 oz (225 g) **Extra-lean ground beef (96% lean)**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 4 cups (120 g) **Baby spinach**
- 1/2 cup (120 g) **No-salt-added diced tomatoes**, drained
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Kosher salt**
- 2 tbsp (14 g) **Feta cheese**, crumbled
- 1 pouch (8.8 oz / 250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa**

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C. Place the mushroom caps gill-side up on a baking sheet, brush with olive oil, and bake for 10 to 12 minutes until tender.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add the ground beef, minced garlic, and kosher salt.
3. Break the meat apart with a spoon and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until browned.
4. Stir the spinach, diced tomatoes, and dried oregano into the skillet. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the spinach is fully wilted.
5. Warm the quinoa pouch in the microwave according to the package instructions.
6. Remove the mushrooms from the oven and carefully drain any excess liquid that pooled inside the caps.
7. Spoon the hot beef and spinach mixture into the mushroom caps. Top with the crumbled feta and serve alongside the warm quinoa.

## Variation
Swap the ground beef for extra-lean ground turkey breast.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in the oven or microwave.
