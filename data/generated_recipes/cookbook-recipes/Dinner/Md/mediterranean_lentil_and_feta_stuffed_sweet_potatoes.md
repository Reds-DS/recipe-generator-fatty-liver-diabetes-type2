# Mediterranean Lentil and Feta Stuffed Sweet Potatoes

![Mediterranean Lentil and Feta Stuffed Sweet Potatoes](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\mediterranean_lentil_and_feta_stuffed_sweet_potatoes.png)

This meatless meal delivers a lasting hit of protein and fiber to keep your energy steady. Microwaving the sweet potatoes creates a fast weeknight base, cleverly stacked with lentils, Greek yogurt, and walnuts to satisfy without heavy prep.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 8–11 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 (250 g) **Small sweet potatoes**, scrubbed
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cumin**
- 7 oz (200 g) **No-salt-added canned lentils**, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups (60 g) **Baby spinach**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Plain nonfat Greek yogurt**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 1.5 oz (42 g) **Feta cheese**, crumbled
- 1 oz (28 g) **Walnuts**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Prick the sweet potatoes all over with a fork. Place on a microwave-safe plate and microwave on High for 6 to 8 minutes, turning halfway, until the centers are completely tender.
2. While the potatoes microwave, heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Add the garlic and cumin; stir for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the lentils and spinach to the skillet. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, tossing frequently, until the spinach is fully wilted and the lentils are heated through. Remove from the heat.
4. Whisk the Greek yogurt and lemon juice together in a small bowl until smooth.
5. Split the hot sweet potatoes open down the center. Divide the lentil and spinach mixture evenly, stuffing it inside and over the top of the potatoes.
6. Drizzle the yogurt sauce over each portion, then sprinkle evenly with the feta cheese and walnuts.

## Variation
Trade the chopped walnuts for toasted pumpkin or sunflower seeds.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat potato and lentils before topping.
