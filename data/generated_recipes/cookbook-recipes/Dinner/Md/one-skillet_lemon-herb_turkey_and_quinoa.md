# One-Skillet Lemon-Herb Turkey and Quinoa

![One-Skillet Lemon-Herb Turkey and Quinoa](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\one-skillet_lemon-herb_turkey_and_quinoa.png)

Bright lemon and savory browned turkey flavor this simple Mediterranean skillet. Simmering the quinoa directly in the broth builds rich flavor and adds fiber using just one pan.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 17–23 min
- **Prep time:** 7 min
- **Rest time:** 2 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 10 oz (283 g) **Lean ground turkey (93% lean)**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Fresh garlic**, minced
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 2/3 cup (113 g) **Quinoa**, rinsed and drained
- 1 1/3 cups (315 ml) **Low-sodium chicken broth**
- 1 medium (200 g) **Zucchini**, diced
- 1 cup (150 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**
- 1 medium (50 g) **Fresh lemon**, zested and juiced
- 2 cups (60 g) **Baby spinach**

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add the turkey, breaking it apart with a spatula, and cook until browned, 4 to 6 minutes.
3. Stir in the garlic and oregano and cook until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes.
4. Stir in the quinoa, broth, zucchini, tomatoes, and salt, then bring to a rolling boil.
5. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer until the liquid is absorbed and the quinoa is tender, 12 to 15 minutes.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat, then fold in the spinach, lemon zest, and lemon juice.
7. Cover the skillet and let stand off the heat until the spinach wilts before serving.

## Variation
Swap the zucchini for diced red or green bell pepper.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently in the microwave.
