# Spanish-Style Butter Bean and Quinoa Egg Skillet

![Spanish-Style Butter Bean and Quinoa Egg Skillet](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\spanish-style_butter_bean_and_quinoa_egg_skillet.png)

A steady delivery of fiber and lean protein helps maintain lasting fullness, making this robust one-pan dinner a smart weeknight choice.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 9–12 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 3 cloves **Garlic**, minced
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1 cup (240 g) **No-salt-added crushed tomatoes**
- 9 oz (260 g) **No-salt-added canned butter beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Red wine vinegar**
- 1 cup (150 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa pouch**
- 2 loosely packed cups (60 g) **Fresh baby spinach**
- 4 **Large eggs**
- 1/2 cup (115 g) **Plain nonfat Greek yogurt**
- 2 tbsp (8 g) **Fresh parsley**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Add minced garlic and smoked paprika, stirring constantly for 30 seconds until fragrant.
2. Pour in crushed tomatoes, butter beans, and red wine vinegar. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.
3. Fold quinoa and baby spinach into the simmering sauce. Stir continuously for 2 to 3 minutes until the spinach wilts.
4. Form small, deep wells in the bean and quinoa mixture using the back of a spoon. Crack eggs into the wells.
5. Cover the skillet tightly with a lid and reduce the heat to medium-low. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes, checking at 5 minutes, until the egg whites are firmly set but the yolks remain soft.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat. Dollop Greek yogurt around the edges and scatter chopped parsley over the top before serving in shallow bowls.

## Variation
Swap the butter beans for low-sodium canned chickpeas.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; soft-cooked eggs do not reheat well.
