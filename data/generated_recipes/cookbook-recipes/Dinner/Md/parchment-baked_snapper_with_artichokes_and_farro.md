# Parchment-Baked Snapper with Artichokes and Farro

![Parchment-Baked Snapper with Artichokes and Farro](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\parchment-baked_snapper_with_artichokes_and_farro.png)

This parchment packet technique creates a fully composed dinner with zero pans to wash. Red snapper steams alongside a bed of Mediterranean vegetables and hearty farro.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 12–15 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 10.6 oz (300 g) **Fresh or frozen red snapper fillets, thawed**
- 7 oz (200 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked farro pouch, unsalted**
- 5.3 oz (150 g) **No-salt-added canned artichoke hearts**, rinsed, drained, and quartered
- 5.3 oz (150 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 1 oz (30 g) **Kalamata olives, pitted**, halved
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Fresh garlic**, minced
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1 medium (60 g) **Fresh lemon**, cut in half

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C. Cut two large rectangles of parchment paper and lay them flat on a baking sheet.
2. Toss the farro, artichoke hearts, cherry tomatoes, olives, minced garlic, olive oil, and dried oregano together in a mixing bowl.
3. Divide the farro and vegetable mixture evenly and mound it onto the center of each parchment sheet.
4. Place the snapper fillets directly on top of each mound.
5. Squeeze half of the lemon over the fillets, then slice the remaining lemon and arrange the slices over the fish.
6. Fold the long sides of each parchment paper up and crimp the edges tightly to seal them into airtight packets.
7. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the fish is opaque and flakes easily with a fork. Open the hot packets carefully and serve.

## Variation
Swap the red snapper for peeled and deveined medium shrimp.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated in an airtight container; reheat gently.
