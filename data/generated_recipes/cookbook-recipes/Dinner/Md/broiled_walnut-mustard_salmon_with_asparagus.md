# Broiled Walnut-Mustard Salmon with Asparagus

![Broiled Walnut-Mustard Salmon with Asparagus](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\broiled_walnut-mustard_salmon_with_asparagus.png)

Perfect for a quick weeknight dinner, this meal uses the broiler to cook everything on a single pan. The crunchy walnut coating provides omega-3s while keeping the fish tender.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 6–8 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 16 oz (450 g) **Fresh asparagus**, woody ends trimmed
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) **Salt**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 8 oz (225 g) **Fresh salmon fillets**, cut into two portions
- 1 tbsp (15 g) **Whole-grain mustard**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 3 tbsp (20 g) **Walnuts**, finely chopped
- 8.8 oz (250 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked brown rice pouch**

## Instructions
1. Preheat the broiler to high (500°F / 260°C) with a rack in the upper third of the oven. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil.
2. Toss the trimmed asparagus directly on the baking sheet with the olive oil, half the salt, and half the pepper. Push the asparagus to one side of the pan.
3. Place the salmon fillets on the empty side of the baking sheet. Mix the mustard and lemon juice in a small bowl, then spread the mixture evenly over the top of the salmon.
4. Press the chopped walnuts into the mustard layer on each fillet. Sprinkle the remaining salt and pepper over the fish.
5. Broil for 6 to 8 minutes until the asparagus is tender, the walnuts are toasted, and the salmon flakes easily with a fork. Check the pan at 6 minutes to ensure the nuts do not burn.
6. Heat the brown rice pouch in the microwave according to the package instructions while the fish cooks.
7. Divide the warm brown rice, roasted asparagus, and walnut-crusted salmon evenly between two plates.

## Variation
Swap the asparagus spears for fresh trimmed green beans.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently in the microwave.
