# Skillet Turkey Puttanesca with Zucchini and Quinoa

![Skillet Turkey Puttanesca with Zucchini and Quinoa](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dinner\IMG\skillet_turkey_puttanesca_with_zucchini_and_quinoa.png)

This simple, one-pan Mediterranean meal comes together rapidly thanks to a few briny pantry staples. Quick-cooking turkey cutlets and a ready-to-eat quinoa pouch keep the active cooking fast and the total time well under thirty minutes.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 13–18 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 9 oz (255 g) **Turkey breast cutlets**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 10 oz (300 g) **Zucchini**, diced
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic**, minced
- 14.5 oz (411 g) **No-salt-added diced tomatoes**, undrained
- 1 oz (28 g) **Kalamata olives**, pitted and halved
- 1 tbsp (9 g) **Capers**, rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Red wine vinegar**
- 8.5 oz (240 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked quinoa pouch**
- 1/4 cup (6 g) **Fresh basil**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Season turkey cutlets on both sides with black pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add turkey to the skillet and cook for 4 to 6 minutes total, flipping halfway, until browned. Check at 4 minutes, then transfer turkey to a plate.
3. Lower heat to medium. Add zucchini to the skillet and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until slightly softened. Stir in garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Pour diced tomatoes with their juices, olives, capers, and red wine vinegar into the skillet. Bring mixture to a rolling simmer.
5. Return turkey cutlets to the skillet, nestling them into the sauce. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 5 to 7 minutes until turkey is cooked through and sauce thickens slightly. Check at 5 minutes.
6. Tear a small vent in the top of the quinoa pouch and microwave on high for 90 seconds until steaming hot.
7. Divide hot quinoa between two plates. Top with turkey, spoon zucchini puttanesca sauce over meat, and sprinkle with fresh basil.

## Variation
Swap the turkey breast for quick-cooking chicken breast tenders.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; reheat gently on the stovetop or microwave.
