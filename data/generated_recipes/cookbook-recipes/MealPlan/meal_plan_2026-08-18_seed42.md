# 30-Day Plan — The Fatty Liver Diet Cookbook for Type 2 Diabetes

**Objective**: One way of eating for both conditions: 30 days of Mediterranean-style meals from everyday supermarket ingredients, energy-controlled for steady weight loss, very low in added sugar, fiber- and protein-forward, and alcohol-free — built to help lower liver fat and keep blood sugar steady.
**Actual average**: 1769 kcal/day

> **Before you start, talk to your doctor or diabetes nurse.** This plan changes what and how much you eat, and if you take insulin, a sulfonylurea, an SGLT2 inhibitor or a GLP-1 medicine, your dose may need adjusting. It averages about 119 g of protein a day, which is more than someone with kidney disease is usually advised to eat, and it is not designed for pregnancy or breastfeeding. It is food, not treatment, and does not replace your medication.
**Dessert is optional** — if you are not hungry, skip it; do not force yourself to eat on schedule. A day without it still comes to about 1579 kcal, 111 g protein and 36 g fiber.
