# Rosemary Almond and Pepita Mix with Pear

![Rosemary Almond and Pepita Mix with Pear](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\rosemary_almond_and_pepita_mix_with_pear.png)

This fast and simple snack requires just a single skillet. Raw almonds and pumpkin seeds are toasted with savory herbs and served alongside fresh pear for an enjoyable crunch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp (25 g) **Raw whole almonds**
- 3 tbsp (25 g) **Raw pumpkin seeds (pepitas)**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Fresh rosemary**, finely chopped
- 1/4 tsp (0.7 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/8 tsp (0.3 g) **Kosher salt**
- 1 medium (180 g) **Fresh pear**, cored and sliced

## Instructions
1. Combine the almonds, pumpkin seeds, olive oil, rosemary, garlic powder, and salt in a small bowl and stir until evenly coated.
2. Heat a medium dry skillet over medium heat.
3. Add the nut mixture to the skillet and toast for 4 to 6 minutes. Stir frequently, checking at 4 minutes, until the seeds begin to pop and smell fragrant.
4. Remove the skillet from the heat and immediately transfer the hot nut mixture to a plate to prevent burning.
5. Divide the sliced pear between two plates and serve alongside the warm toasted nuts.

## Variation
Swap the sliced pear for a crisp Granny Smith apple.

## Storage
Store leftover cooled nuts up to 3 days at room temperature.
