# Smoky Butter Bean Dip with Sweet Peppers

![Smoky Butter Bean Dip with Sweet Peppers](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\smoky_butter_bean_dip_with_sweet_peppers.png)

Silky and boldly spiced, this creamy dip uses tender butter beans to create a satisfying, high-fiber snack.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 5 oz (150 g) **Canned no-salt-added butter beans**, rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Water**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (10 g) **Unsalted roasted pumpkin seeds (pepitas)**
- 1 clove (3 g) **Garlic**, peeled and smashed
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Smoked paprika**
- 1/8 tsp (0.5 g) **Salt**
- 3 oz (80 g) **Red bell pepper**, sliced into strips
- 2 oz (60 g) **Carrots**, cut into sticks

## Instructions
1. Place the butter beans, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, smoked paprika, and salt in a food processor or blender.
2. Blend on high speed until completely smooth and creamy, pulsing in the water if the mixture is too thick.
3. Transfer the dip to a serving bowl.
4. Garnish the surface with the roasted pumpkin seeds.
5. Serve immediately alongside the red bell pepper and carrots for dipping.

## Variation
Trade the smoked paprika for ground cumin and fresh cilantro.

## Storage
Keeps up to 3 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
