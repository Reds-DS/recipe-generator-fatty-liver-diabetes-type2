# Hummus, Crispbread, and Vegetable Snack Plates

![Hummus, Crispbread, and Vegetable Snack Plates](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\hummus,_crispbread,_and_vegetable_snack_plates.png)

A balance of plant protein, healthy fats, and complex carbohydrates provides steady afternoon energy without a blood sugar spike. Plenty of fresh vegetables and whole grains deliver lasting fullness.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup (80 g) **Store-bought traditional hummus**
- 2 slices (18 g) **Whole-grain crispbreads**
- 1 cup (100 g) **English cucumber**, sliced
- 2/3 cup (100 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 2 tbsp (18 g) **Raw unsalted almonds**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Za'atar seasoning or dried oregano**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Lemon juice**

## Instructions
1. Divide the hummus evenly between two snack plates or airtight meal-prep containers.
2. Arrange one crispbread slice, the prepared cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and almonds around the hummus on each plate.
3. Sprinkle the za'atar or oregano evenly over the hummus and the fresh vegetables.
4. Drizzle the lemon juice lightly over the vegetables just before serving.

## Variation
Swap the almonds for raw unsalted walnuts or pumpkin seeds.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
