# Roasted Red Pepper and Walnut Dip

![Roasted Red Pepper and Walnut Dip](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\roasted_red_pepper_and_walnut_dip.png)

An ideal mid-afternoon snack to bridge the gap until dinner, this Mediterranean spread recreates the rich flavors of muhammara without the wait. Walnuts lend a creamy body to the dip while providing supportive omega-3 fatty acids.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup (120 g) **Jarred roasted red peppers, no-salt-added**, rinsed and drained well
- 1/3 cup (40 g) **Shelled raw walnuts**
- 1 clove (3 g) **Fresh garlic**, peeled
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Ground cumin**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Red pepper flakes**
- 1/8 tsp (0.5 g) **Salt**
- 1 small pita (50 g) **Whole-wheat pita bread**, cut into wedges
- 1 medium (200 g) **Cucumber**, sliced into thick rounds

## Instructions
1. Place the drained roasted red peppers, walnuts, garlic, lemon juice, extra-virgin olive oil, cumin, red pepper flakes, and salt into a small food processor or blender.
2. Blend on high speed until smooth, scraping down the sides of the bowl with a spatula as needed.
3. Transfer the dip to a small serving bowl.
4. Arrange the cucumber rounds and whole-wheat pita wedges on a plate alongside the dip.
5. Serve immediately using the cucumber and pita to scoop up the dip.

## Variation
Swap the cucumber slices for crisp celery or carrot sticks.

## Storage
Keeps 3-4 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
