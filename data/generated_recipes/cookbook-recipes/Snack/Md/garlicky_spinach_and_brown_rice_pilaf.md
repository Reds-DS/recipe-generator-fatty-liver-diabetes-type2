# Garlicky Spinach and Brown Rice Pilaf

![Garlicky Spinach and Brown Rice Pilaf](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\garlicky_spinach_and_brown_rice_pilaf.png)

Providing steady complex carbohydrates and fiber, this quick side dish helps keep energy levels stable between meals. It transforms simple brown rice with fresh spinach, bright lemon, and crunchy walnuts into an effortless, vegetable-heavy plate.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 2 cloves (6 g) **Garlic cloves**, minced
- 6 oz (170 g) **Fresh baby spinach**, roughly chopped
- 1 cup (150 g) **Ready-to-eat cooked brown rice pouch**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Lemon zest**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Lemon juice**
- 2 tbsp (14 g) **Chopped walnuts**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**

## Instructions
1. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat until shimmering.
2. Add the garlic and sauté for 30 to 60 seconds until fragrant but not browned. Check at 30 seconds.
3. Add the baby spinach in handfuls, tossing constantly for 1 to 2 minutes until fully wilted. Check at 1 minute.
4. Stir in the cooked brown rice, breaking up any clumps. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until heated through. Check at 2 minutes.
5. Remove the skillet from the heat.
6. Stir in the lemon zest, lemon juice, chopped walnuts, and black pepper.
7. Divide the pilaf between plates and serve warm.

## Variation
Swap the chopped walnuts for toasted pine nuts or almonds.

## Storage
Keeps 2 to 3 days refrigerated; reheat in the microwave.
