# Lemony Black-Eyed Pea and Tomato Salad

![Lemony Black-Eyed Pea and Tomato Salad](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\lemony_black-eyed_pea_and_tomato_salad.png)

When you need a fast afternoon snack or a vegetable side for a weeknight dinner, this crisp marinated bean salad works perfectly. The lemon and oregano dressing pairs easily with grilled or roasted mains.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Rest time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 cup (160 g) **Canned no-salt-added black-eyed peas**, rinsed and drained
- 1/2 cup (100 g) **Cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 1/2 cup (100 g) **Cucumber**, diced
- 1/4 cup (30 g) **Red onion**, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup (10 g) **Fresh parsley**, chopped
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Lemon zest**
- 1 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1/8 tsp (0.7 g) **Salt**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, oregano, salt, and black pepper together in a medium bowl.
2. Add the black-eyed peas, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and parsley to the bowl.
3. Toss the salad until all ingredients are coated in the dressing.

## Variation
Swap the fresh parsley for chopped fresh dill or mint.

## Storage
Keeps 2 to 3 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
