# Chili-Lime Edamame and Sweet Corn Salad

![Chili-Lime Edamame and Sweet Corn Salad](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\chili-lime_edamame_and_sweet_corn_salad.png)

Forget waiting for a pot to boil; combining two colorful freezer staples with a bright citrus dressing creates a satisfying, high-fiber side without the stove.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 7 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 cup (155 g) **Frozen shelled edamame**
- 1/2 cup (82 g) **Frozen sweet corn kernels**
- 1/4 cup (40 g) **Red onion**, finely chopped
- 1.5 tsp (7 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lime juice**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Chili powder**
- 2 tbsp (8 g) **Fresh cilantro**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Place the edamame and sweet corn in a microwave-safe bowl.
2. Microwave on high for 2 to 3 minutes, checking halfway, until both vegetables are completely thawed and just warmed through.
3. Drain any excess water from the bowl to prevent a soggy salad.
4. Add the red onion, olive oil, lime juice, and chili powder to the warmed vegetables.
5. Toss everything well to coat the edamame and corn evenly in the dressing.
6. Stir in the chopped cilantro just before serving or storing.

## Variation
Swap the chili powder for ground cumin and smoked paprika.

## Storage
Keeps 2 to 3 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
