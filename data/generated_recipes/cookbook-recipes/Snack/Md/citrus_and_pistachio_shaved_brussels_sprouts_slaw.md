# Citrus and Pistachio Shaved Brussels Sprouts Slaw

![Citrus and Pistachio Shaved Brussels Sprouts Slaw](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\citrus_and_pistachio_shaved_brussels_sprouts_slaw.png)

This simple no-cook slaw requires minimal prep and uses convenient ingredients. Pre-shaved Brussels sprouts tenderize perfectly when tossed with fresh orange segments and a tangy vinaigrette.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Rest time:** 5-10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Apple cider vinegar**
- 1 tsp (5 g) **Dijon mustard**
- 1/8 tsp (0.75 g) **Salt**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**
- 1 medium (130 g) **Fresh orange**, peeled
- 7 oz (200 g) **Bagged shaved Brussels sprouts**
- 1 oz (30 g) **Shelled pistachios**, roughly chopped

## Instructions
1. Whisk the extra-virgin olive oil, apple cider vinegar, Dijon mustard, salt, and black pepper in a large mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Cut the peeled orange into bite-sized segments and discard any seeds.
3. Add the shaved Brussels sprouts and orange segments to the bowl with the dressing.
4. Toss the salad thoroughly and massage the Brussels sprouts to help them absorb the liquid and soften.
5. Chop the pistachios.
6. Sprinkle the chopped pistachios over the slaw before serving.

## Variation
Swap the orange segments for diced crisp green apple.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; add pistachios just before serving.
