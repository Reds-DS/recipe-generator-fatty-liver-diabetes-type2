# Lemon-Dill Shrimp and Cucumber Bites

![Lemon-Dill Shrimp and Cucumber Bites](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\lemon-dill_shrimp_and_cucumber_bites.png)

Provides a satisfying hit of lean protein to keep energy steady and easily bridge the gap between meals. Thawing ready-to-eat shrimp under cold water creates an instant, refreshing plate with plenty of crisp crunch.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 4 oz (115 g) **Frozen cooked shrimp (untreated, peeled, tail-off)**
- 5 oz (150 g) **English cucumber**, sliced into thick rounds
- 1.3 oz (38 g) **Low-sodium whole-wheat woven crackers**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Fresh lemon juice**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (3 g) **Fresh dill**, finely chopped
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**

## Instructions
1. Place the frozen shrimp in a colander and run under cold water for 5 to 7 minutes until completely thawed.
2. Transfer the thawed shrimp to paper towels and pat them thoroughly dry.
3. Chop the shrimp roughly into small, bite-sized pieces so they will easily balance on the crackers.
4. Transfer the chopped shrimp to a small bowl and toss with the lemon juice, olive oil, fresh dill, and black pepper.
5. Arrange the crackers on a serving plate.
6. Top each cracker with a cucumber round and a spoonful of the herbed shrimp mixture.

## Variation
Swap the dill for chopped fresh cilantro and a lime squeeze.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away to keep the crackers completely crisp.
