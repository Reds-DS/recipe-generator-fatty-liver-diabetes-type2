# Mediterranean Lentil Salad Stuffed Mini Peppers

![Mediterranean Lentil Salad Stuffed Mini Peppers](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\mediterranean_lentil_salad_stuffed_mini_peppers.png)

Using sweet pepper halves as edible scoops turns a hearty, fiber-rich lentil salad into a satisfying finger food.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 15 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 8 peppers (120 g) **Mini sweet peppers**
- 8.5 oz (240 g) **No-salt-added canned brown lentils**, rinsed and drained
- 2.8 oz (80 g) **Fresh cucumber**, finely diced
- 0.7 oz (20 g) **Kalamata olives**, finely chopped
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Red wine vinegar**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Dried oregano**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Black pepper**

## Instructions
1. Halve the mini sweet peppers lengthwise and scoop out the seeds and membranes to create hollow boats.
2. Whisk the olive oil, red wine vinegar, dried oregano, and black pepper in a medium mixing bowl.
3. Fold the lentils, cucumber, and olives into the dressing and toss to coat the ingredients evenly.
4. Spoon the lentil mixture into the hollowed sweet pepper halves and serve.

## Variation
Swap the dried oregano for freshly chopped mint or dill.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; store the peppers and filling separately.
