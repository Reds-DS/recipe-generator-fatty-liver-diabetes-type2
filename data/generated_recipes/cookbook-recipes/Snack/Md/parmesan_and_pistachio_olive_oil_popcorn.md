# Parmesan and Pistachio Olive Oil Popcorn

![Parmesan and Pistachio Olive Oil Popcorn](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\parmesan_and_pistachio_olive_oil_popcorn.png)

Bringing Mediterranean ingredients to a movie-night classic turns a simple whole grain into a savory, fiber-rich snack. Popping the corn in olive oil and tossing it with crushed pistachios delivers a satisfying crunch that supports steady blood sugar.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 3–5 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup (30 g) **Shelled pistachios**, roughly chopped
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1/4 cup (50 g) **Popcorn kernels**
- 3 tbsp (15 g) **Grated parmesan cheese**
- 1/2 tsp (2 g) **Garlic powder**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Dried thyme**
- 1/4 tsp (1 g) **Black pepper**

## Instructions
1. Heat the extra-virgin olive oil in a large pot with a tight-fitting lid over medium-high heat. Drop in the test popcorn kernels.
2. Add the remaining popcorn kernels when the test kernels pop. Cover the pot and remove from the heat for 30 seconds to equalize the temperature.
3. Return the pot to medium heat.
4. Shake the pot continuously as the corn pops for 2 to 4 minutes. Check the progress at 2 minutes, and stop shaking when the popping slows to one pop every 2 to 3 seconds.
5. Remove the pot from the heat immediately. Add the chopped pistachios, grated parmesan cheese, garlic powder, dried thyme, and black pepper directly into the hot pot.
6. Toss the mixture well so the residual heat warms the nuts and helps the cheese stick.
7. Transfer the popcorn to a large bowl and serve warm.

## Variation
Trade the dried thyme for a pinch of smoked paprika.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
