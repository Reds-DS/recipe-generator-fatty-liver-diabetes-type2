# Warm Cinnamon-Berry Greek Yogurt Bowls with Toasted Oats

![Warm Cinnamon-Berry Greek Yogurt Bowls with Toasted Oats](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Snack\IMG\warm_cinnamon-berry_greek_yogurt_bowls_with_toasted_oats.png)

This simple six-ingredient snack is completely ready in under ten minutes. A warm compote of frozen mixed berries and cinnamon perfectly complements chilled nonfat Greek yogurt and freshly toasted oats.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 3–5 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups (340 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 1 cup (140 g) **Frozen mixed berries**
- 1/4 cup (20 g) **Old-fashioned rolled oats**
- 1 1/2 tbsp (10 g) **Sliced almonds**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Ground cinnamon**
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) **Vanilla extract**

## Instructions
1. Place a small, dry skillet over medium heat. Add the oats and sliced almonds.
2. Toast the mixture, stirring frequently, for 3 to 5 minutes until golden and fragrant. Check at 3 minutes to prevent burning, then transfer to a small dish to cool.
3. Place the frozen berries in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave on high for 1 to 2 minutes until hot and juicy, checking at 1 minute.
4. Mash the warm berries slightly with a fork to release their natural syrup. Stir in the cinnamon and vanilla extract.
5. Divide the yogurt between two serving bowls.
6. Spoon the warm berry compote over the yogurt and top with the toasted oat and almond crunch.

## Variation
Swap the mixed berries for frozen dark sweet cherries.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
