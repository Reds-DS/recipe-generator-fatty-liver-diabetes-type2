# Warm Lemon Custard with Fresh Raspberries

![Warm Lemon Custard with Fresh Raspberries](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\warm_lemon_custard_with_fresh_raspberries.png)

This remarkably easy dessert relies on a brief stovetop technique to build a luxurious texture with minimal effort. You will whisk low-fat milk, an egg, and a touch of cornstarch into a warm, creamy base before topping it with fresh raspberries.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 4–6 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1 1/4 cups (305 g) **Low-fat milk (1%)**
- 1 large (50 g) **Large egg**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Pure maple syrup**
- 1 tbsp (8 g) **Cornstarch**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Lemon zest**, finely grated
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Pure vanilla extract**
- 1 cup (123 g) **Fresh raspberries**

## Instructions
1. In a medium saucepan, vigorously whisk the milk, egg, maple syrup, and cornstarch off the heat until entirely smooth.
2. Place the saucepan on the stovetop over medium heat.
3. Whisk constantly for 4 to 6 minutes, until the mixture begins to gently bubble and significantly thickens into a pudding-like consistency. Check at 4 minutes.
4. Remove the saucepan from the heat immediately.
5. Stir in the finely grated lemon zest and vanilla extract.
6. Divide the warm custard evenly between two serving bowls and top with the fresh raspberries.

## Variation
Swap the raspberries for sliced fresh strawberries or whole blackberries.

## Storage
Keeps 2-3 days refrigerated; enjoy cold or gently reheat.
