# Warm Berry and Almond Skillet Crisp

![Warm Berry and Almond Skillet Crisp](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\warm_berry_and_almond_skillet_crisp.png)

For a cozy weeknight dessert, this quick stovetop crumble hits all the right comfort notes. Lightly toasting the oats and nuts adds a satisfying crunch over tangy Greek yogurt and naturally sweet fruit.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 7–11 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 6 1/2 oz (180 g) **Frozen mixed berries**
- 3/4 oz (20 g) **Old-fashioned rolled oats**
- 3/4 oz (20 g) **Sliced almonds**
- 1/2 tbsp (7 ml) **Extra-virgin olive oil**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cinnamon**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Pure maple syrup**
- 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) **Pure vanilla extract**
- 4 1/4 oz (120 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**

## Instructions
1. Combine the oats, almonds, and extra-virgin olive oil in a small skillet over medium heat.
2. Toast the mixture for 4 to 6 minutes, stirring frequently, until the oats are golden and fragrant. Transfer the oat mixture to a small bowl and set aside.
3. Return the skillet to medium heat and add the frozen berries, cinnamon, and maple syrup. Simmer for 3 to 5 minutes, mashing slightly with a spoon, until the fruit softens and the juices begin to bubble and thicken. Check at 3 minutes.
4. Remove the skillet from the heat and stir the vanilla extract directly into the warm berries.
5. Divide the Greek yogurt between two serving bowls, spoon the warm fruit over the yogurt, and top evenly with the toasted crisp mixture.

## Variation
Swap the sliced almonds for chopped pecans or walnuts.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; the crisp topping does not keep.
