# Almond Butter and Cocoa Stuffed Dates

![Almond Butter and Cocoa Stuffed Dates](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\almond_butter_and_cocoa_stuffed_dates.png)

Relying on whole fruit for sweetness provides steady energy without a sugar crash. These Medjool dates deliver a rich chocolate center alongside satisfying fiber and lean protein.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 large (48 g) **Medjool dates**, pitted and halved lengthwise
- 2 tbsp (32 g) **Natural almond butter**
- 2 tsp (4 g) **Unsweetened cocoa powder**
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 2 tbsp (14 g) **Chopped walnuts**, chopped
- 1/8 tsp (0.5 g) **Flaky sea salt**

## Instructions
1. Arrange the date halves cut-side up on a small plate. Press the centers to widen the cavities.
2. Mix the almond butter, cocoa powder, and vanilla extract in a small bowl until the ingredients form a thick paste.
3. Divide the almond-cocoa mixture evenly among the date halves. Press the filling into the hollowed centers.
4. Sprinkle the chopped walnuts over the filled dates. Press the nuts into the almond butter so they stick.
5. Top each piece with flaky sea salt before serving.

## Variation
Swap the chopped walnuts for raw unsalted pumpkin seeds.

## Storage
Keeps 4 to 5 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
