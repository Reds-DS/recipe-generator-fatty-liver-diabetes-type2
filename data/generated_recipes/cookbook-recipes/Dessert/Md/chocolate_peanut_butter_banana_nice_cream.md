# Chocolate Peanut Butter Banana Nice Cream

![Chocolate Peanut Butter Banana Nice Cream](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\chocolate_peanut_butter_banana_nice_cream.png)

This frozen treat delivers the thick texture of soft-serve ice cream using simple everyday staples. It satisfies sweet cravings naturally with whole fruit, rich cocoa, and a steady supply of protein.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 5.3 oz (150 g) **Frozen bananas**, frozen and sliced
- 1/3 cup (75 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) **Unsweetened almond milk**
- 1 tbsp (16 g) **Natural creamy peanut butter**
- 1 tbsp (5 g) **Unsweetened cocoa powder**
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 2 tsp (10 g) **Dark chocolate chips**

## Instructions
1. Place the frozen banana slices, Greek yogurt, almond milk, peanut butter, cocoa powder, and vanilla extract into a food processor or blender.
2. Pulse the ingredients to break down the frozen fruit, then blend on high speed.
3. Stop the machine and scrape down the sides with a rubber spatula to integrate the ingredients.
4. Blend until the mixture is smooth, thick, and resembles soft-serve ice cream.
5. Divide the mixture evenly between two serving bowls.
6. Sprinkle the dark chocolate chips over the top of each bowl.
7. Serve immediately while chilled.

## Variation
Swap the peanut butter for natural almond butter.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
