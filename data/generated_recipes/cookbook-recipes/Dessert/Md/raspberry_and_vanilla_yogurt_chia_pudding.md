# Raspberry and Vanilla Yogurt Chia Pudding

![Raspberry and Vanilla Yogurt Chia Pudding](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\raspberry_and_vanilla_yogurt_chia_pudding.png)

This refreshing make-ahead dessert is ready in the fridge whenever you need a creamy treat. Chia seeds and Greek yogurt create a spoonable base layered with bright berries.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Chill time:** 2 hours
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp (30 g) **Chia seeds**
- 3/4 cup (180 ml) **Unsweetened soy milk**
- 1/2 cup (115 g) **Plain nonfat Greek yogurt**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Pure maple syrup**
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 1 cup (120 g) **Fresh raspberries**, rinsed and dried
- 1 tbsp (8 g) **Sliced almonds**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the chia seeds, soy milk, Greek yogurt, maple syrup, and vanilla extract together in a medium bowl until smooth.
2. Let the mixture sit for 5 minutes, then whisk it again to prevent the seeds from clumping.
3. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight to allow the chia seeds to hydrate.
4. Divide the thickened pudding between serving glasses or bowls.
5. Top the pudding with the raspberries and sliced almonds before serving.

## Variation
Swap the fresh raspberries for diced fresh peaches or strawberries.

## Storage
Keeps up to 3 days in an airtight container refrigerated.
