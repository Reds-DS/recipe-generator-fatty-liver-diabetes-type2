# Warm Pumpkin Spice Oat Mug Cakes

![Warm Pumpkin Spice Oat Mug Cakes](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\warm_pumpkin_spice_oat_mug_cakes.png)

This simple dessert requires minimal prep and uses the microwave to bake quickly. Canned pure pumpkin and oat flour create a cozy, spiced cake topped with cool Greek yogurt.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 2–4 min
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Rest time:** 2 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup (80 g) **Canned pure pumpkin**
- 1 (50 g) **Large egg**
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) **Pure maple syrup**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 1/3 cup (32 g) **Oat flour**
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Pumpkin pie spice**
- 1/2 tsp (2 g) **Baking powder**
- 1 tbsp (8 g) **Chopped pecans**, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup (60 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**

## Instructions
1. Whisk the pumpkin, egg, maple syrup, and vanilla extract in a small bowl until completely smooth.
2. Stir the oat flour, pumpkin pie spice, and baking powder into the wet ingredients until just combined into a thick batter.
3. Divide the batter evenly between two microwave-safe mugs or small ramekins.
4. Microwave one mug on high power for 1 to 2 minutes, checking at 1 minute, until the cake is puffed and the center is set.
5. Repeat the microwave process with the second mug, then let both rest.
6. Top each warm cake with the Greek yogurt and pecans before serving.

## Variation
Swap the chopped pecans for finely chopped walnuts.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
