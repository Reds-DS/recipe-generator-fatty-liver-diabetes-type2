# Broiled Cinnamon Peaches with Vanilla Ricotta Cream

![Broiled Cinnamon Peaches with Vanilla Ricotta Cream](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\broiled_cinnamon_peaches_with_vanilla_ricotta_cream.png)

With minimal prep, this warm dessert is ready in under 15 minutes. Broiling fresh peaches concentrates their natural juices, pairing well with the vanilla ricotta and crunchy almonds.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 5–7 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 10.5 oz (300 g) **Fresh peaches**, halved and pitted
- 1/4 cup (60 g) **Part-skim ricotta cheese**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) **Ground cinnamon**
- 2 tbsp (15 g) **Sliced almonds**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Pure maple syrup**

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven broiler to High (500°F / 260°C) for 3 minutes. Line a baking sheet with foil.
2. Place the peaches cut-side up on the prepared baking sheet. Dust evenly with the cinnamon.
3. Broil for 5 to 7 minutes, checking at 5 minutes, until the fruit is tender and the edges are lightly golden.
4. Whisk the ricotta cheese and vanilla extract in a bowl until smooth and creamy.
5. Toast the sliced almonds in a dry skillet over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, shaking the pan frequently, until lightly browned and fragrant.
6. Divide the warm peaches between serving plates. Top each half with the vanilla ricotta, maple syrup, and toasted almonds.

## Variation
Swap the fresh peaches for halved and cored fresh plums.

## Storage
Best enjoyed right away; does not keep.
