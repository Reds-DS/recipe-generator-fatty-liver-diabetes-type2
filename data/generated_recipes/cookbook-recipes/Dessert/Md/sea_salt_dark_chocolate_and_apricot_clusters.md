# Sea Salt Dark Chocolate and Apricot Clusters

![Sea Salt Dark Chocolate and Apricot Clusters](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\sea_salt_dark_chocolate_and_apricot_clusters.png)

When you want a satisfying after-dinner treat or an afternoon pick-me-up, these bites deliver a candy-like experience without the sugar crash. Dried fruit and crunchy nuts bind together with dark chocolate to create a perfect salty-sweet balance.

## Details
- **Cook time:** none (no-cook)
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Freeze time:** 10-15 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 oz (55 g) **Unsweetened dried apricots**, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp (20 g) **Dry-roasted unsalted almonds**, roughly chopped
- 1 tbsp (10 g) **Dry-roasted pumpkin seeds**
- 1 tbsp (12 g) **Dark chocolate chips (70% cocoa or higher)**
- 1/8 tsp (0.5 g) **Flaky sea salt**

## Instructions
1. Line a small plate or baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Combine the finely chopped apricots, almonds, and pumpkin seeds in a small bowl and toss to mix.
3. Place the dark chocolate chips in a small microwave-safe bowl and microwave on high in 15-second bursts, stirring between each, until completely smooth.
4. Pour the melted chocolate over the apricot and nut mixture, stirring until all pieces are coated.
5. Scoop the mixture into clusters on the prepared plate and press them with the back of a spoon so they hold together.
6. Sprinkle the clusters evenly with the flaky sea salt.
7. Place the plate in the freezer to set until firm, about 10 to 15 minutes.

## Variation
Swap the dried apricots for unsweetened dried cherries or figs.

## Storage
Keeps 1 week refrigerated in an airtight container.
