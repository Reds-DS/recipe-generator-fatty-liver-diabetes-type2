# Cardamom-Poached Pears with Pistachio Yogurt

![Cardamom-Poached Pears with Pistachio Yogurt](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\cardamom-poached_pears_with_pistachio_yogurt.png)

Swapping heavy wine syrups for an aromatic poaching liquid lets the true flavor of the fruit shine. Paired with thick yogurt, it creates an elegant, blood-sugar-friendly finish to any meal.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 10–12 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 2 small (260 g) **Firm pears**, peeled, halved lengthwise, and cored
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) **Water**
- 1 stick (2 g) **Cinnamon stick**
- 3 pods (1 g) **Cardamom pods**, lightly crushed
- 2 wide strips (4 g) **Orange peel**
- 1/2 cup (115 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 2 tsp (10 ml) **Pure maple syrup**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 1 tbsp (8 g) **Shelled pistachios**, roughly chopped

## Instructions
1. Combine the water, cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, and orange peel in a small saucepan and bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat.
2. Reduce the heat to medium-low and add the pear halves to the saucepan, cut-side down.
3. Cover with a lid and simmer for 10 to 12 minutes until the pears are tender when pierced with a fork.
4. Whisk the Greek yogurt, maple syrup, and vanilla extract in a small bowl until smooth while the pears poach.
5. Remove the saucepan from the heat and transfer the poached pears to a plate to cool.
6. Divide the sweetened yogurt between shallow bowls and spread it into an even layer.
7. Arrange the warm pear halves over the yogurt in each bowl and sprinkle with the chopped pistachios before serving.

## Variation
Swap the pistachios for toasted slivered almonds or chopped walnuts.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat pears gently in the microwave.
