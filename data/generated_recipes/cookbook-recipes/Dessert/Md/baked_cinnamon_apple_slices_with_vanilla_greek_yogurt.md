# Baked Cinnamon Apple Slices with Vanilla Greek Yogurt

![Baked Cinnamon Apple Slices with Vanilla Greek Yogurt](C:\Users\terja\Documents\AI_Projects\recipe-generator-fatty-liver-diabetes\data\generated_recipes\cookbook-recipes\Dessert\IMG\baked_cinnamon_apple_slices_with_vanilla_greek_yogurt.png)

This incredibly simple, fast-prep dessert requires almost no active time. Warm, spice-roasted apples deliver comforting flavor over a protein-rich base of vanilla Greek yogurt.

## Details
- **Cook time:** 15–20 min
- **Prep time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 2

## Ingredients
- 9 oz (250 g) **Fresh apple**, cored and sliced 1/4-inch thick
- 1 tsp (2 g) **Ground cinnamon**
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) **Ground nutmeg**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Maple syrup**
- 5.5 oz (150 g) **Nonfat plain Greek yogurt**
- 1 tsp (5 ml) **Vanilla extract**
- 2 tbsp (15 g) **Pecans**, chopped

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Toss the sliced apples with the cinnamon, nutmeg, and maple syrup in a bowl until evenly coated.
3. Spread the apples in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
4. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes, or until the apples are tender and lightly browned. Check at 15 minutes.
5. Stir the vanilla extract into the Greek yogurt while the apples bake.
6. Divide the vanilla yogurt between two small bowls and top warmly with the baked apple slices.
7. Sprinkle the chopped pecans over the apples and serve immediately.

## Variation
Swap the chopped pecans for sliced almonds or toasted walnuts.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat apples slightly before serving.
